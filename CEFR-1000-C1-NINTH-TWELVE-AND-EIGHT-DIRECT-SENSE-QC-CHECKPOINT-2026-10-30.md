## Eight directly opened Oxford dictionary sense leads

These are **publisher source observations only**, not independent human verification.

- [discipline](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/discipline_1), lines 151–175: uncountable personal self-control is distinct from imposed discipline and an academic field. Draft learning-language example targets self-control.
- [unemployment](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/unemployment), lines 117–173: a population-level social/economic condition or number/rate, relevant to unemployment among graduates. Draft Myanmar wording should not imply that the noun itself always means the rate.
- [vulnerable](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/vulnerable), lines 103–116: susceptibility to harm; the draft's extreme-heat context is plausible, but needs independently checked Myanmar nuance.
- [chronic](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/chronic), lines 105–156: long-term illness/condition, distinct from informal British 'very bad'. The dictionary's **Oxford 3000 B2** badge conflicts with the directly inspected **Oxford 5000 C1** PDF listing.
- [insight](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insight), lines 105–205: 'insight into' means understanding, aligned with the survey example. Dictionary's **Oxford 5000 C1** badge conflicts with **Oxford 3000 B2** PDF.
- [transaction](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/transaction), lines 105–173: countable buying/selling or business transaction, distinct from formal uncountable 'transaction of business'. Dictionary **Oxford 3000 B2** badge conflicts with **Oxford 5000 C1** PDF.
- [thereby](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/thereby), lines 103–142: formal result marker; `thereby lowering costs` is a natural consequence construction and is not merely `furthermore`.
- [slavery](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/slavery), lines 103–205: uncountable condition/institution of forced ownership and labour; dictionary **Oxford 3000 B2** badge conflicts with **Oxford 5000 C1** PDF.

The four PDF-versus-dictionary badge conflicts must remain visible; neither source surface can be silently preferred as independent exact-sense CEFR approval.
