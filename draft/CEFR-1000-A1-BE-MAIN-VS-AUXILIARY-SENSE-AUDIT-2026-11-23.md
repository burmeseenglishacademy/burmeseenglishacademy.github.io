## Evidence and exact-sense boundary
1. Cambridge Essential British English, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/be (observed 2026-11-23), explicitly marks main verb **A1** for description/identity, existence (`there is/are`) and location; auxiliary **A2** for continuous and passive constructions. This is a dictionary-specific sense classification, not a blanket CEFR license for every `be` construction.
2. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/be?q=be_1 (observed 2026-11-23), independently distinguishes A1 descriptive/existence/position uses from A2 auxiliary progressive constructions.
3. British Council LearnEnglish, https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/present-simple-be (observed 2026-11-23), provides beginner-level `am/is/are` affirmative, negative, question and contraction patterns; this grammar lesson supports usage but does not independently classify every lexical sense.
4. Cambridge English Grammar Today, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/be- (observed 2026-11-23), distinguishes main verb from auxiliary; this is grammatical evidence, not CEFR grading.

## Editorial risk
The original English gloss blends existence, identity and qualities, all plausible A1 main-verb uses, but its example specifically illustrates identity/job after `want to`, not existence. Myanmar `ဖြစ်သည်၊ ရှိသည်` is broad and context-dependent; a learner might incorrectly apply `ရှိသည်` to job identity or `ဖြစ်သည်` to location. Do not teach the A2 progressive/passive auxiliary as automatically A1 merely because the headword is A1. Do not infer CEFR from a grammar page alone.

## Candidate author-side refinement (NOT independently checked)
- Target sense: **be (main verb):** to describe who or what someone/something is, what it is like, or where it is; also in `there is/are` to say something exists. Prefer separate usage notes or sub-senses if the final manifest supports them.
- Myanmar contextual note: လူ/အရာ၏ အမည်၊ အလုပ်အကိုင်၊ အခြေအနေ၊ တည်နေရာကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် `am/is/are` ကို သုံးသည်။ `there is/there are` ဖြင့် တစ်စုံတစ်ရာ ရှိကြောင်း ဖော်ပြသည်။ (`ဖြစ်သည်` / `ရှိသည်` ကို ဝါကျအလိုက် ရွေးသုံးရန်။)
- Original examples, not copied from sources:
  - "My sister is a nurse." — "ကျွန်တော့်/ကျွန်မရဲ့ အစ်မက သူနာပြုတစ်ယောက်ပါ။" (identity/job; kinship and speaker-register variants require Myanmar review)
  - "The keys are on the desk." — "သော့တွေက စားပွဲပေါ်မှာ ရှိတယ်။" (location)

- The original "I want to be a teacher." is grammatical but should not stand alone as evidence for all three uses; if retained, make the specific identity/job sense explicit.
