## A1 — but (conjunction)
- Shortlist: `draft/CEFR-1000-PROVISIONAL-SHORTLIST.json`, A1; source `draft/A1-EDITORIAL-STARTER.json` index 35, blob `ff1e93f60828ef2f94c9146522ce94075183b0ae`.
- Current first pass: “Used to join ideas that contrast.” / “ဒါပေမယ့်၊ သို့သော်” / “I am tired, but I am happy.”
- Source: Cambridge Grammar Today, *Conjunctions: contrasting*, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting (observed 2026-11-17). Cambridge distinguishes coordinating **but** from subordinating **although/though**, and cautions against treating them as interchangeable in clause structure. This is grammatical evidence, **not** a CEFR A1 level label.
- Proposed exact-sense definition: “A conjunction joining two contrasting words, phrases or clauses.”
- Proposed Myanmar: “ဆန့်ကျင်ဘက် သို့မဟုတ် မျှော်လင့်ထားသည်နှင့် ကွဲပြားသော အကြောင်းအရာနှစ်ခုကို ဆက်ရာတွင် သုံးသော ‘ဒါပေမဲ့/သို့သော်’ ဟူသည့် ဆက်စပ်စကားလုံး။” The natural register of ဒါပေမဲ့ vs ဒါပေမယ့် needs independent Myanmar signoff.
- Original example candidate: “The room is small, but it is comfortable.” Myanmar: “အခန်းက သေးပေမယ့် နေလို့အဆင်ပြေတယ်။”
- Review risks: distinguish **but** coordinating clauses from **though** subordinating clauses; verify the exact sense's A1 label with a source that actually assigns CEFR; check punctuation and fluent Myanmar.

## A2 — though (conjunction)
- Shortlist: A2; source `draft/A2-C1-EDITORIAL-STARTER.json` index 509, blob `b04dd85db5cfae131c25aa4c73109778991b6fdc`.
- Current first pass: “Used to introduce a fact that contrasts with another fact.” / “သော်လည်း၊ ပေမယ့်” / “Though the shop was busy, the staff were helpful.”
- Source: same Cambridge Grammar Today page (observed 2026-11-17). Cambridge says **though/although** are subordinating conjunctions, may introduce a first or final subordinate clause, and **though** is more common in speech. This does **not** substantiate an exact-sense A2 label.
- Proposed exact-sense definition: “Introduces a subordinate clause giving a contrast to the main clause.”
- Proposed Myanmar: “အဓိကဝါကျနှင့် ဆန့်ကျင်နေသော အခြေအနေတစ်ခုကို ‘…ပေမယ့်/…သော်လည်း’ ဟု ဖော်ပြရန် သုံးသော ဆက်စပ်စကားလုံး။”
- Original example candidate: “Though it was raining, we walked to the station.” Myanmar: “မိုးရွာနေပေမယ့် ကျွန်တော်တို့ ဘူတာကို လမ်းလျှောက်သွားခဲ့တယ်။”
- Review risks: **though** can also be a sentence-final adverb (“It was nice, though”), a different grammatical sense not covered by this shortlist entry; never transfer its CEFR label. Independently confirm A2 for this conjunction sense or hold/reassign it. Do not insert redundant **but** into the main clause after an initial **though** clause.
