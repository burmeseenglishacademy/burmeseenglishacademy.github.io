## Editorial issue: sense contamination
The current definition conflates a beginner **linking verb** (“She is a teacher”; “The room is quiet”) with independent **existential `be`** (“to exist”), a sense that is not demonstrated by the present shortlist example. The current example “I want to be a teacher” illustrates infinitival identity/occupation, not existence. Do **not** infer that the existential sense is A1 merely because the British Council teaches present-simple `am/is/are` at A1/A2. Likewise, auxiliary `be` in “She is reading” and passive `be` in “The letter was sent” require separate grammatical treatment and must not be silently included under the proposed sense.
