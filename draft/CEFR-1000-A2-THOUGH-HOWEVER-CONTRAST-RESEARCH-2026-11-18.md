## Specific issues to resolve

- **A2 `though` conflict:** The current example is a concessive conjunction introducing a full clause; the cited British Council teaching unit is B1–B2. This is a **level-evidence gap**, not proof the A2 assignment is false. Require independent exact-sense A2 evidence or hold/reassign the sense; do not infer A2 from a broad contrast-word family.
- **Part-of-speech split:** Clause-initial `though` is a conjunction; sentence-final `though` functions as an adverb/discourse marker. Do not collapse them under one conjunction entry or use an adverb example to certify the conjunction.
- **`however` syntax/register:** The existing semicolon and comma correctly separate independent clauses. A bare comma splice (*The room was small, however, it was comfortable*) must not be proposed. The Myanmar `ဒါပေမဲ့` is conversational, whereas `သို့သော်` better represents a more formal linking adverb. Neither translation alone conveys punctuation/register.
- **Cross-level conceptual overlap:** `though` and `however` both express contrast but have different grammatical distributions; headword-level deduplication must not erase the distinction. The presence of both at A2 is not itself independent evidence that both are A2.
