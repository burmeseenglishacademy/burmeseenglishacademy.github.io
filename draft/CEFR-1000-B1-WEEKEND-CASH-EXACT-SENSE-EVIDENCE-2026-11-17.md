## 1. weekend — provisional B1 noun, source_index 12
- Shortlist exact sense: Saturday and Sunday, when many people do not work. Existing Myanmar: `သီတင်းပတ်ကုန်`. Existing example: “We visited our grandparents at the weekend.”
- **Direct conflict:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary assigns **A1** to this exact noun sense, not B1: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/learner-english/weekend . Cambridge English Dictionary also labels the same weekend noun sense A1: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/weekend .
- Editorial candidate: “Saturday and Sunday, the days at the end of the week.” Myanmar: `စနေ၊ တနင်္ဂနွေပါဝင်သော သီတင်းပတ်ကုန်`. New original example: “We usually visit our grandparents at the weekend.” British English `at the weekend` is supported by the Cambridge Learner's entry; do not replace with an American-only phrasing.

## 2. cash — provisional B1 noun, source_index 14
- Shortlist exact sense: money in the form of notes and coins. Existing Myanmar: `ငွေသား`. Existing example: “The small shop accepts cash only.”
- **Direct conflict and polysemy:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary explicitly assigns **A2** to the physical coins/notes noun sense, while **B1** refers to a broader “money in any form” sense: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/cash . Cambridge Essential British English independently distinguishes A2 physical cash from B1 informal broader money: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/cash .
- Editorial candidate for the **existing** sense: “Money you can hold, such as banknotes and coins, rather than a bank card.” Myanmar: `ငွေစက္ကူနှင့် အကြွေစေ့တို့ဖြင့် ရှိသော ငွေသား`. New original example: “This market stall accepts cash but not bank cards.”
- If a B1 sense is sought, the definition and Myanmar meaning must change to reflect informal money/funds generally, e.g. `သုံးစွဲရန်ရှိသော ငွေ`; a B1 example candidate is “The charity is short of cash this month.” This is a **different sense**; it cannot inherit A2 evidence or be substituted without explicit independent review.
- **Action:** Flag exact-sense CEFR mismatch; independent reviewer to resolve B1 inventory placement, sense and Myanmar fluency.
