## Observed dictionary evidence (22 November 2026)
1. Cambridge Learner’s Dictionary, [prepare](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/prepare), explicitly labels the **get someone/something ready** sense **A2**, the **food preparation** sense **A2**, and the reflexive **prepare yourself** for difficulty sense **B1**.
2. Cambridge Essential British English, [prepare](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/prepare), likewise labels the general readying and food senses **A2**, and the difficult-situation reflexive sense **B1**.
3. Cambridge English Dictionary, [prepare](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/prepare), labels the general readying sense **A2** and the anticipation/readiness sense **B1**.

**Exact-sense comparison:** preparing documents before a meeting is ordinary preparation of materials, directly aligned with the dictionaries’ **A2 general readying** sense, not a distinct B2 sense. A workplace context alone does not raise the lexical sense to B2. No cited source supports B2 for this provisional sense. This is an evidence-backed **placement conflict**, not an independently decided reclassification.
