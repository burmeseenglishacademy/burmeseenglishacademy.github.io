## Editorial and release risks
- **Do not confuse determiner and pronoun:** “That bag is mine” = determiner; “That is mine” = pronoun. A1 label for one sense cannot automatically be transferred to the other.
- **Do not confuse complementizer/relative *that*:** “I think that…” and “the book that…” are distinct grammatical functions.
- **Do not conflate physical distance and discourse distance:** identify the exact sense and require a CEFR-tagged publisher entry or equivalent level-specific evidence before any A1 claim.
