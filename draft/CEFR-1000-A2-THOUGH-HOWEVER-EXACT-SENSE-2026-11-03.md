## 1. though — provisional A2, conjunction, historical source index 509

Frozen definition: “Used to introduce a fact that contrasts with another fact.” Frozen Myanmar: “သော်လည်း၊ ပေမယ့်”. Frozen original example: “Though the shop was busy, the staff were helpful.”

**Observed publisher evidence (2026-11-03):** Cambridge English Dictionary `though` lists the subordinating conjunction meaning *despite the fact that* at **B1**, the separate conjunction meaning *but* at **B2**, and sentence-final adverb *despite this* at **B2**. URL: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/though?topic=connecting-words-which-express-a-contrast (publisher-indexed page content inspected on 2026-11-03). Cambridge Grammar Today separately confirms the subordinate-clause grammar and distinguishes sentence-final adverb: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/though (publisher page indexed, inspected 2026-11-03).

**Decision lead:** The frozen example is a concessive subordinate clause and aligns with the Cambridge **B1 conjunction** sense, not the provisional A2. Do not reclassify automatically: independent reviewer must adjudicate exact sense and replacement quotas. The original English sentence is grammatical; initial concessive clause is correctly followed by a comma. Myanmar `သော်လည်း` is appropriate in formal written contexts, while `ပေမယ့်` is conversational; both need independent fluent-Myanmar signoff. Proposed teaching alternative (NOT APPROVED): “Though the bus was late, we arrived on time.” / “ဘတ်စ်ကား နောက်ကျခဲ့ပေမယ့် ကျွန်တော်တို့ အချိန်မီ ရောက်ခဲ့ကြတယ်။” The alternative adds person/register assumptions and needs review.

## 2. however — provisional A2, adverb, historical source index 285

Frozen definition: “Used to introduce a fact that contrasts with what was just said.” Frozen Myanmar: “သို့သော်၊ ဒါပေမဲ့”. Frozen original example: “The room was small; however, it was comfortable.”

**Observed publisher evidence (2026-11-03):** Cambridge publisher-owned bilingual dictionary indexed the contrastive **adverb** `however` as **A2**, meaning *despite this*, with UK pronunciation /ˌhaʊˈev.ə(r)/. Confirm against the monolingual exact sense before human approval. Cambridge Grammar Today notes `however` is not interchangeable with the coordinating conjunction `but`: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting (inspected 2026-11-03).

**Decision lead:** Provisional A2 appears plausible for the contrastive adverb, but not independently verified. Frozen example is grammatical: semicolon joins two independent clauses, comma follows the connective adverb. Myanmar `သို့သော်` is written/formal, `ဒါပေမဲ့` conversational; these must not be presented as identical in register. Proposed teaching alternative (NOT APPROVED): “The task was difficult.
