## Source-supported orthographic defect
The first-person singular subject pronoun must be capitalised **I**, even when it appears in the middle of a sentence. The lowercase headword `i` in the shortlist is therefore a spelling/orthography defect; the example already correctly uses uppercase `I`. This is a **headword display correction candidate**, not a reason to silently change the immutable intake or treat the item as independently reviewed.

Sources consulted on **2026-11-17**:
1. Madonna University Writing Center, ESL Grammar Reference, capitalization and pronoun lists: https://www.madonna.edu/resources/writing-center/online-tutoring/ESL-Grammar.pdf — directly identifies `I` as capitalised and a first-person pronoun.
2. Abingdon School Grammar Handbook, §3.6: https://www.abingdon.org.uk/uploads/prep/files/academic/2015/2015-16_grammar_handbook.pdf — explicit rule that the first-person pronoun `I` takes a capital.

These sources support the orthographic rule, **not an independently established CEFR A1 level**. A1 remains provisional pending an appropriate level-and-sense reference.
