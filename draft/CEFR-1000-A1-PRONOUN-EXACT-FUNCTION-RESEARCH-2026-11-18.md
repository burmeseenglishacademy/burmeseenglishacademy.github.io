## Entry-specific first-pass proposals (not approved)

### A1 source index 3 — stored `i` (pronoun)
- **Issue:** headword must be uppercase `I` in standard written English. This is an orthographic correction, not a different lexical sense. Existing example `I am a student.` is grammatical.
- **Narrow English definition candidate:** “The subject pronoun a speaker or writer uses to refer to themselves.”
- **Myanmar candidate:** “စကားပြောသူ သို့မဟုတ် စာရေးသူက မိမိကိုယ်ကို ရည်ညွှန်းရာတွင် ဝါကျ၏ ကတ္တားအဖြစ် သုံးသော နာမ်စား (ကျွန်တော်၊ ကျွန်မ)။”
- **New original example:** “I work at a small shop.”
- **Reviewer decisions needed:** confirm written headword `I`, subject role, appropriate Myanmar register, A1 exact-sense evidence and audio display/synthesis handling.

### A1 source index 4 — `you` (pronoun)
- **Issue:** current “person or people someone is speaking to” is broad enough for address but fails to distinguish subject vs object and singular vs plural. Existing `You are my friend.` illustrates singular subject only.
- **Narrow English definition candidate:** “The pronoun used for the person or people being addressed; it can be a subject or an object.”
- **Myanmar candidate:** “စကားပြောနေသူက မိမိပြောဆိုနေသော လူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် လူအများကို ရည်ညွှန်းသည့် နာမ်စား။ အခြေအနေအလိုက် ‘မင်း၊ သင်၊ ရှင်၊ ခင်ဗျား၊ သင်တို့’ စသည်ဖြင့် ပြန်ဆိုနိုင်သည်။”
- **New original examples:** “You can sit here.” (subject); “I will call you tomorrow.” (object).
- **Reviewer decisions needed:** whether a single headword covers both grammatical functions under the 200-entry constraint; register sensitivity (avoid implying `မင်း` is always polite); do not silently merge generic `you` with direct address without evidence.

### A1 source index 5 — `we` (pronoun)
- **Issue:** `we` includes the speaker plus at least one other person; the listener may or may not be included. Distinguish `we` from object `us`. A Myanmar gloss limited to `ကျွန်ုပ်တို့` can be formal or unnatural in ordinary speech.
- **Narrow English definition candidate:** “The subject pronoun for the speaker or writer together with one or more other people.”
- **Myanmar candidate:** “စကားပြောသူ သို့မဟုတ် စာရေးသူနှင့် အခြားသူတစ်ဦးဦး ပါဝင်သော အုပ်စုကို ဝါကျ၏ ကတ္တားအဖြစ် ရည်ညွှန်းသည့် နာမ်စား (ကျွန်တော်တို့၊ ကျွန်မတို့၊ ငါတို့ စသည်)။”
- **New original example:** “We eat lunch together.”
- **Reviewer decisions needed:** inclusive/exclusive context, natural register, and whether `we` needs separate sense annotations for different contexts.
