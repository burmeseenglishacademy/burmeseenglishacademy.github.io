## B1 independent — unresolved sense/level mismatch

Shortlist: adjective; source index 11; English definition **“Not controlled by another person or organisation.”**; Myanmar **“အမှီအခိုကင်းသော၊ လွတ်လပ်သော”**; example **“The team made an independent decision.”**; review task `CEFR1000-B1-0012` (verify task ID against task manifest before editorial intake).

Cambridge Learner's Dictionary separates **NEED (B1)**, meaning not relying on help, from **RULE (B2)**, meaning not controlled or ruled by others, and **INFLUENCE (B2)**, meaning not influenced by others. The existing definition directly resembles RULE (B2), while the example about a decision resembles INFLUENCE (B2), rather than the B1 NEED sense. The existing Myanmar wording combines self-reliance and freedom/autonomy, concealing this distinction.

Sources (consulted 2026-11-20):
- https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/independent — explicitly B1 NEED; B2 RULE and INFLUENCE.
- https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/independent — explicitly B1 NOT HELPED; B2 NOT INFLUENCED and NOT RULED.

**Editorial alternatives, not changes:** If retaining B1, select the self-reliance sense, e.g. English definition “Able to do things without relying on other people's help”; Myanmar “သူတစ်ပါးအကူအညီကို မမှီခိုဘဲ ကိုယ့်ဘာသာ လုပ်ကိုင်နိုင်သော”; original example “My grandfather is independent and cooks his own meals.”; Myanmar example “ကျွန်တော့်အဘိုးက သူများကို မမှီခိုဘဲ ကိုယ်တိုင် ထမင်းဟင်းချက်စားနိုင်တယ်။” (speaker-specific “ကျွန်တော့်” should be replaced by neutral phrasing if gender neutrality is desired: “အဘိုးက သူများကို မမှီခိုဘဲ ကိုယ်တိုင် ထမင်းဟင်းချက်စားနိုင်တယ်။”). Alternatively retain the decision-making sense but independently investigate a B2 move and rebalance exactly 200 entries per level. Do not silently reassign.

**Reviewer must determine:** exact sense; whether original example tests that sense; natural Myanmar register; B1/B2 level with source-backed justification; and potential collision if any replacement word is already present elsewhere.

Cambridge Essential British English and Learner's Dictionary both label the inquiry/curiosity verb sense **B1**, distinguishing it from the noun of admiration and other uses. This supports, but does not independently approve, the B1 shortlist placement. Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary corroborates verb meaning and the grammatical pattern `wonder + wh-clause`, without establishing an explicit CEFR rating.
