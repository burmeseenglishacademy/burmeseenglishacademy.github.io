## Editorial problem and candidate correction
The provisional definition, “To exist or to have a particular quality or identity,” merges existential and descriptive functions. The example “I want to be a teacher.” specifically illustrates a future job/identity, not the existential sense. Myanmar `ဖြစ်သည်၊ ရှိသည်` similarly merges functions. The entry may remain one headword only if the reviewer formally specifies its target sense and does not treat the other functions as approved automatically.

**Suggested exact sense:** linking verb expressing identity, occupation, or quality (A1 descriptive sense).

**Candidate English definition (original):** Used to say who or what someone or something is, or to describe their condition.

**Candidate Myanmar meaning (original):** လူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် အရာဝတ္ထုတစ်ခု၏ အမည်၊ အမျိုးအစား၊ အလုပ်အကိုင် သို့မဟုတ် အခြေအနေကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် သုံးသော ကြိယာ။ (`ဖြစ်သည်` ဟု အမြဲ တိုက်ရိုက်မပြန်ဆိုရ။)

**Candidate English example (original):** My sister is a nurse.

**Candidate Myanmar translation (original):** ကျွန်တော့်အစ်မက သူနာပြုတစ်ယောက်ပါ။ (Speaker-specific `ကျွန်တော့်` must be adapted to `ကျွန်မရဲ့` for a female speaker; a gender-neutral teaching alternative is `ကျွန်ုပ်၏အစ်မသည် သူနာပြုတစ်ဦးဖြစ်သည်။`.)

**Grammar check:** singular third-person subject `My sister` takes `is`; noun complement `a nurse` is countable singular and requires `a`. This example targets the linking/occupation sense, not auxiliary `be`.
