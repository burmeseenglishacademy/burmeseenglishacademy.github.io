Proposed candidate for the strict sense: “အသေးစိတ်အချက်အလက်အားလုံး တစ်ထပ်တည်းတူညီသော”. **Not independently verified**.
- The existing example is grammatically acceptable but semantically nuanced: “looked identical” expresses visual appearance, whereas the missing signature means the documents were **not actually identical in every detail**. It illustrates an apparent resemblance, not an unambiguous absolute identity.
