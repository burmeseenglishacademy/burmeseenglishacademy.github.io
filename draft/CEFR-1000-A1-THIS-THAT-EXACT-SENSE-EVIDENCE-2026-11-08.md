## Publisher observations and sense boundaries
1. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, [this](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this), observed 2026-11-08: **determiner NEAR = A1**; determiner referring to previously discussed information and present/next time = A1. The **pronoun** sense referring to previously discussed information is marked **A2**. UK /ðɪs/; plural *these*. Therefore do not transfer a determiner's A1 evidence indiscriminately to all pronoun uses.
2. Cambridge Essential British English, [this](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/this), observed 2026-11-08: separately lists **A1 determiner NEAR**, and **A2 pronoun NEAR** (e.g. introducing someone). This is a particularly important POS-specific boundary.
3. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, [that](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/that), observed 2026-11-08: **determiner NOT NEAR = A1**, previously discussed/seen determiner = A1; **pronoun NOT NEAR = A1**. UK /ðæt/; plural *those*. This differs from *this*'s A2 pronoun treatment; do not assume symmetry.
4. Cambridge Essential British English, [that](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/that), observed 2026-11-08: confirms **A1 determiner NOT NEAR** and previously discussed senses, plus A1 pronoun senses. Anaphoric connective *that* is A2; these classifications cannot be borrowed for the inventory's demonstrative determiner.
5. Cambridge English Dictionary, [that](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/that), observed 2026-11-08: marks demonstrative determiner A1 and adverbial degree use (*that far*) **B2**; relative/conjunction uses require separate evidence. Its combined broad headword presentation is less precise than the learner/essential POS entries.

## Editorial proposals, not applied
- *this* exact sense: “Used before a noun to identify a person or thing near the speaker or being pointed at.” Myanmar teaching explanation: “ပြောသူနဲ့ နီးတဲ့၊ ဒါမှမဟုတ် လက်ညှိုးထိုးပြနေတဲ့ လူတစ်ယောက် သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ရည်ညွှန်းရာမှာ နာမ်ရှေ့က သုံးတဲ့ စကားလုံး။” Compact gloss: “ဒီ၊ ဤ”.
- Original example: “This chair is next to my desk.” / “ဒီကုလားထိုင်က ကျွန်တော့်စားပွဲဘေးမှာ ရှိတယ်။”
- *that* exact sense: “Used before a noun to identify a person or thing farther from the speaker.” Myanmar teaching explanation: “ပြောသူနဲ့ အလှမ်းဝေးတဲ့ လူတစ်ယောက် သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ရည်ညွှန်းရာမှာ နာမ်ရှေ့က သုံးတဲ့ စကားလုံး။” Compact gloss: “ဟို၊ အဲဒီ”.
- Original example: “That shop across the road is closed.” / “လမ်းတစ်ဖက်က ဟိုဆိုင် ပိတ်ထားတယ်။”
- Grammar: *this/that + singular countable noun*; *these/those + plural noun*. Do not treat `this is...` / `that is...` as determiner examples; those are pronoun uses. Context is essential for Burmese near/far demonstratives, and “အဲဒီ” can also refer to previously mentioned entities.
- British listening checkpoints: /ðɪs/ versus /ðæt/, initial voiced /ð/, short /ɪ/ versus /æ/, plural /ðiːz/ versus /ðəʊz/. These are linguistic notes only; no George/Emma recording or listening signoff exists here.
