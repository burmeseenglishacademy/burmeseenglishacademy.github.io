## Specific reviewer decisions required
- `yes`: distinguish affirmative answer from enthusiasm (“Yes!”) and agreement; ensure dialogue actually demonstrates affirmative response.
- `no`: distinguish independent reply/interjection from the **determiner** in “no money”; these are different parts of speech/senses and cannot inherit the same CEFR evidence.
- `please`: distinguish polite-request marker from verb `please` (“to make someone happy”) and acceptance (“Yes, please”); verify the apparent PALSO level-placement discrepancy rather than silently ignoring it.
- `hello`: verify greeting sense rather than attention-getting telephone usage if dictionary labels differ.
- `goodbye`: confirm whether the approved lexical count treats `goodbye` and `good-bye` as spelling variants, not separate words.
- Myanmar register: `မင်း` is informal; reviewer should confirm target audience and alternatives, especially classroom and older-addressee speech. `ပိတ်ပေးပါနော်` expresses polite request without literal word-for-word `please` translation.
