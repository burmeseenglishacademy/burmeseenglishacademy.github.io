2. Cambridge Essential British English Dictionary, `signature` noun, **B1** for the same handwritten-name sense. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/signature (observed 2026-11-23).
3. Cambridge English Dictionary, `signature` noun (NAME), **B1**, the name a person writes to identify or agree to something. Distinguishes an uncountable formal 'act of signing' sense without an observed B1 label for that separate sense. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/signature?topic=signing-and-signatures (observed 2026-11-23).
4. Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary independently corroborates the handwritten-name meaning, but its entry is **not** taken as CEFR level certification. https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/signature (observed 2026-11-23).

**Finding:** the precise provisional C1 handwritten-name meaning has explicit **B1** evidence in multiple Cambridge learner references. It must not be represented as source-backed C1. Do not substitute the distinct 'characteristic identifying feature', formal signing-act, or adjective senses to salvage a C1 slot.
