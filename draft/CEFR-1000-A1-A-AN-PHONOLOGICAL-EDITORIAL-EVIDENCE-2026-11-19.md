## Source-backed editorial analysis
Cambridge English Grammar Today explains that `a` and `an` are alternative forms of the indefinite article, used for a non-identified referent. **The following spoken sound**, not the written first letter, determines the form: `a university` and `an hour` are instructive counterexamples. The existing `a` definition omits its consonant-sound condition and neither definition specifies the normal singular-countable-noun environment. The existing examples are grammatical but do not test spelling-versus-sound exceptions. This is a **definition-completeness issue**, not a claim that the current examples are incorrect.
