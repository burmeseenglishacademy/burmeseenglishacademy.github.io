## B2 hide — task CEFR1000-B2-0627, global ordinal 627, source_index 141

Provisional verb definition: “To put something where other people cannot see it.” Myanmar: `ဝှက်ထားသည်၊ ဖုံးကွယ်သည်`. Example: “Please do not hide important documents.”

**Observed external evidence:** Cambridge Essential British English labels the put-something-out-of-sight verb sense **B1**, not B2: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/hide (observed 2026-11-23). Cambridge English Dictionary also labels the core concealment verb **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/hide (observed 2026-11-23). The Learner's Dictionary additionally distinguishes concealment of an object, concealment of oneself, and concealment of feelings/information, each marked B1: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/de/worterbuch/learner-englisch/hide (observed 2026-11-23).

**Sense and bilingual risk:** The existing example could mean physically concealing documents or withholding information. Myanmar `ဝှက်ထားသည်` fits hiding a physical object; `ဖုံးကွယ်သည်` may also imply concealment of information. An independent reviewer must choose one intended sense, specify it, and revise the example if necessary. Author-only candidate for physical-object sense: English “To put an object somewhere so that people cannot find or see it”; Myanmar `ပစ္စည်းတစ်ခုကို အခြားသူများ မမြင်နိုင်၊ မတွေ့နိုင်သည့် နေရာတွင် ဝှက်ထားသည်`; original example “She hid the spare key behind a flowerpot.” This is a **B1-aligned candidate**, not evidence for retaining B2.

## B2 height — task CEFR1000-B2-0634, global ordinal 634, source_index 148

Provisional noun definition: “The distance from the bottom to the top of something.” Myanmar: `အမြင့်`. Example: “Please measure the height of the box.”

**Observed external evidence:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary labels the HOW TALL measurement sense **B1**, and the HOW FAR UP sense separately **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/height (observed 2026-11-23). Cambridge Essential British English also marks both **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/height (observed 2026-11-23). Cambridge English Dictionary labels the top-to-bottom distance sense **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/height (observed 2026-11-23).

**Sense and bilingual risk:** `အမြင့်` is usable but too terse to distinguish an object's measured height from elevation above ground. Author-only candidate: English “The measurement from the bottom of an object to its top”; Myanmar `အရာဝတ္ထုတစ်ခု၏ အောက်ခြေမှ အပေါ်ဆုံးအထိ တိုင်းတာထားသော အမြင့်`; original example “The carpenter checked the height of the wooden cabinet before fitting it under the shelf.” The original box example is grammatical and sense-consistent, but **does not support B2 classification**.
