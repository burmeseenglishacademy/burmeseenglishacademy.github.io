## 1. I — A1 subject pronoun
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/i — explicit **A1** for first-person speaker/writer as **subject of the verb**; UK IPA /aɪ/.
- Cambridge English Dictionary: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/i — explicit **A1** for the pronoun; distinguishes letter/number senses from the subject pronoun.
- Cambridge pronunciation: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/i — UK /aɪ/.
- Provisional headword `i` is lowercased. **Correction proposed, not applied:** canonical display headword **I**. Retain case-insensitive duplicate checking; do not count the letter `i` or Roman numeral `I` as this lexical sense.
- Proposed exact-sense English: “The word the speaker or writer uses for themselves when they are the subject of a verb.”
- Proposed fluent Myanmar: “စကားပြောသူ သို့မဟုတ် စာရေးသူက မိမိကိုယ်ကို ဝါကျ၏ ကတ္တားအဖြစ် ရည်ညွှန်းရာတွင် သုံးသော နာမ်စား။”
- Original illustrative example: “I work at a school.” Myanmar: “ကျွန်တော်/ကျွန်မ ကျောင်းတစ်ကျောင်းမှာ အလုပ်လုပ်ပါတယ်။” Pronoun choice depends on speaker and register; do not teach `I` as object form (`me`).
- Provisional “I am a student.” is grammatically sound but the entry's lowercase spelling should not be treated as correct orthography.

## 2. this — A1 proximal demonstrative determiner
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this?q=this_3 — **A1** for determiner referring to something/someone near or pointed at; also separately labels the time determiner sense A1. The **pronoun** sense referring back to a previous topic is listed **A2**, so it must not be silently substituted.
- Cambridge Grammar: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/determiners_2 — identifies `this` as a determiner used before nouns.
- Proposed exact-sense English: “Used before a singular noun or uncountable noun to point to something or someone near the speaker.”
- Proposed fluent Myanmar: “ပြောသူနှင့် နီးသော သို့မဟုတ် လက်ညှိုးထိုးပြနေသော လူ၊ အရာကို ညွှန်းရန် နာမ်ရှေ့တွင် သုံးသော စကားလုံး (ဒီ၊ ဤ)။”
- Original example: “This chair is for you.” Myanmar: “ဒီကုလားထိုင်က မင်းအတွက်ပါ။” `This` modifies `chair`; it is not the stand-alone pronoun sense.
- Provisional “This bag is mine.” is grammatical and compatible with the A1 determiner sense.
