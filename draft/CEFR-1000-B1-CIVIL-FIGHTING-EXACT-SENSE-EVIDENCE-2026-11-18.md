## 1. B1 fighting, noun, source index 167
Current definition: “Physical conflict or an attempt to stop an opponent.” Myanmar: “တိုက်ခိုက်မှု၊ ရန်ဖြစ်မှု”. Example: “The fighting stopped before sunrise.”
**Direct conflict:** Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary labels the *uncountable noun fighting* B2, especially the act of fighting in war, while Cambridge Learner's Dictionary labels *fight* (countable noun physical altercation) B1. These are not interchangeable lemma/POS/countability records. Oxford's fighting entry distinguishes battles, physical struggle, arguments and effort; its topic labels for argument and success do not by themselves certify the current warfare example as B1.

## 2. B1 civil, adjective, source index 51
Current definition: “Connected with ordinary citizens rather than the military.” Myanmar: “အရပ်ဘက်ဆိုင်ရာ”. Current example: “The group works to protect civil rights.”
**Direct conflict:** Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary marks the ordinary/nonmilitary/nonreligious *civil* adjective sense C1, not B1. Oxford separates citizen-related, nonmilitary/state, private-law, and polite senses. The example's collocation *civil rights* refers to citizens' rights and does not cleanly demonstrate a military-versus-civilian contrast. Thus the example is insufficient evidence for the stated exact sense.
Sources observed 2026-11-18:
- https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/civil (C1 ordinary sense)
- https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/civil-rights (separate phrase meaning)
- https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/civil (multiple senses; topic tags not exact-sense certification)
Editorial candidate for the existing stated sense, not replacement: English “Relating to ordinary people or nonmilitary authorities, rather than the armed forces.” Myanmar “စစ်တပ်နှင့် မသက်ဆိုင်ဘဲ အရပ်သားများ သို့မဟုတ် အရပ်ဘက်အာဏာပိုင်များနှင့် သက်ဆိုင်သော”. Original example: “Civil authorities coordinated the rescue with the army.” Myanmar: “အရပ်ဘက်အာဏာပိုင်တွေက ကယ်ဆယ်ရေးလုပ်ငန်းကို စစ်တပ်နဲ့ ညှိနှိုင်းဆောင်ရွက်ခဲ့တယ်။”
