## Evidence and editorial actions

### A2 `team` — task CEFR1000-A2-0007

- Current provisional definition: “A group of people who work or play together.”
- Current Myanmar: “အသင်း၊ အဖွဲ့”
- Current original example: “Our team meets every Monday morning.”
- Cambridge **Learner's Dictionary** distinguishes **SPORT (A2)** from **WORK GROUP (B1)**, instead of assigning one level to all uses of the noun. The current example clearly depicts a work team; it is therefore **not source-supported as A2 by this learner-dictionary sense mapping**.
- Evidence: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/learner-english/team (observed **2026-11-22**, sport A2 / work group B1). Caveat: the separate general English dictionary https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/team labels its broader grouped-people sense A2. The editorial review must identify and record which sense inventory and dictionary edition governs the curriculum, not silently cherry-pick a level.
- Candidate **A2 sport sense**, subject to independent reviewer: “A group of players who compete together against another group.” Myanmar: “အားကစားပြိုင်ပွဲတွင် အတူတကွ ယှဉ်ပြိုင်ကစားသော အသင်း”. New original example: “Our football team won the match on Saturday.”
- Alternative: retain the current work-team example and seek independent **B1** adjudication; do not silently shift this record because exactly 200 approved entries per level are required.
- English/Myanmar risk: “အသင်း၊ အဖွဲ့” alone does not communicate the distinction between sport and workplace. Example is grammatically natural but selects the B1 workplace sense in the Learner's Dictionary.

### B1 `ship` — task CEFR1000-B1-0013

- Current provisional definition: “A large boat used to carry people or goods across water.”
- Current Myanmar: “သင်္ဘော”
- Current original example: “The ship arrived at the port this morning.”
- Cambridge **Learner's Dictionary** gives the large sea-going vessel **noun** sense **A2**, not B1. The separate **verb** “send/transport” is a different part of speech and cannot rescue the noun's B1 placement.
- Evidence: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/learner-english/ship and https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/ship (observed **2026-11-22**, noun boat A2).
- Candidate exact-sense wording: “A large boat that carries people or goods by sea.” Myanmar: “ပင်လယ်ရေကြောင်းဖြင့် လူများ သို့မဟုတ် ကုန်ပစ္စည်းများ သယ်ဆောင်သည့် သင်္ဘောကြီး”. New original example: “The cargo ship reached the harbour before sunrise.”
- **Action:** Flag B1 placement for independent adjudication and possible replacement/rebalancing. Do not assert that the alternative example makes the word B1; it remains A2 in cited evidence. Existing example is grammatical and consistent with noun sense; level is the problem.
