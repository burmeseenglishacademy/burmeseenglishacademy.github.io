## Candidate findings

### 1. civilian — CEFR1000-C1-0829 (shortlist source index 271)

- **Selected sense:** noun, a person who is not a member of the armed forces or police.
- **Source evidence:** Cambridge English Dictionary labels this noun sense **C2**, not C1: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/civilian
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary also gives the civilian noun sense without displaying a CEFR label on the inspected page: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/civilian
- **Level finding:** direct C2-versus-C1 conflict for the selected sense. Do not treat the unlabelled Learner's Dictionary entry as evidence that it is C1. Hold for independent adjudication; likely re-level or replace if the project requires the Cambridge level.
- **Myanmar:** `အရပ်သား` is a concise, natural equivalent for the noun. Keep it distinct from the adjective `civilian` = `အရပ်သားဆိုင်ရာ`.
- **Original example proposal:** “Relief workers delivered clean water to civilians in the affected district.” This illustrates the noun and is not copied from the dictionary.

### 2. coalition — CEFR1000-C1-0830 (shortlist source index 272)

- **Selected sense:** noun, an alliance of political parties or groups formed to act together, commonly for a shared purpose or to govern.
- **Sense evidence:** Cambridge describes the political-party/group alliance sense and gives the collocation “coalition government”; the inspected Cambridge entry displays no CEFR label: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/coalition
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary also defines the political-party sense without a visible CEFR label: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/coalition
- A secondary Wordmom lexical profile assigns the headword B2, but its level methodology is not established in the inspected page; treat this only as a low-confidence warning, not a decisive CEFR source: https://wordmom.com/dictionary/coalition
- **Level finding:** C1 is not established by the authoritative dictionary pages inspected. The secondary B2 signal adds a cross-level risk but is not independently validated. Keep on hold.
- **Definition refinement:** “A temporary alliance of political parties or groups formed to achieve a shared aim or govern together.”
- **Myanmar refinement:** `နိုင်ငံရေးပါတီများ သို့မဟုတ် အဖွဲ့အစည်းများက ရည်မှန်းချက်တစ်ခုအတွက် ပူးပေါင်းဖွဲ့စည်းထားသော မဟာမိတ်အဖွဲ့`.
- **Original example proposal:** “After weeks of negotiation, the smaller parties agreed to join the coalition.”

### 3. controversial — CEFR1000-C1-0831 (shortlist source index 273)

- **Selected sense:** adjective, causing strong disagreement or argument.
- **Source evidence:** Cambridge labels this exact general adjective sense **B2**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/controversial
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary independently shows the same sense and B2 label: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/controversial
- **Level finding:** direct B2-versus-C1 conflict. The current Myanmar meaning `အငြင်းပွားဖွယ်ဖြစ်သော` is broadly accurate, though `သဘောထားကွဲလွဲမှုများ ဖြစ်စေသော` can make the cause-of-disagreement sense clearer. Hold for re-level/replacement decision.
- **Original example proposal:** “The board postponed its decision because the proposal remained controversial.”

### 4. dominant — CEFR1000-C1-0833 (shortlist source index 275)

- **Selected sense:** general adjective, more important, strong or noticeable than others of the same type.
- **Source evidence:** Cambridge labels this general adjective sense **C1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dominant
- The Cambridge entry also has specialised biological, anatomical, psychological and music senses. They must not be merged into this general adjective entry.
- **Level finding:** the inspected C1 label matches the selected general adjective sense. This is useful source-backed author evidence, but it is not independent signoff.
- **Myanmar:** `လွှမ်းမိုးထားသော` is suitable in power/market contexts; `အခြားအရာများထက် ပိုမိုအရေးပါသော` may fit importance or prominence contexts. A single Myanmar gloss should be checked against the chosen example.
- **Existing example assessment:** “One company became dominant in the local market.” Grammatically natural and aligned with the general adjective sense; it is not the Cambridge example.

### 5. furthermore — CEFR1000-C1-0834 (shortlist source index 278)

- **Selected sense:** formal adverb adding another point or, often, a more important point.
- **Source evidence:** Cambridge labels this connective **B2**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/furthermore
- **Level finding:** direct B2-versus-C1 conflict. The Myanmar equivalent `ထို့အပြင်` is natural; note the formal written register. Hold for re-level/replacement decision.
- **Existing example assessment:** “The plan is costly; furthermore, it will take months to finish.” The semicolon and conjunctive-adverb punctuation are correct. The sentence is original-looking, but the lexical level conflict remains.

### 6. treasury — CEFR1000-C1-0822 (shortlist source index 261)

- **Selected sense:** noun, the government department responsible for public finances.
- **Sense evidence:** Collins COBUILD distinguishes the government department from a room for storing valuables, public funds/revenue, and a collection of valued works: https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/treasury
- Cambridge's entry for **the Treasury** describes a named government department responsible for financial matters: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/treasury
- A secondary Wordmom lexical profile labels the headword B2, but does not establish a transparent sense-specific CEFR method: https://wordmom.com/dictionary/treasury
- AccentHero's frequency-based page reports different levels for British and American English (B2 and C1 respectively), which demonstrates that this is not a stable, authoritative sense-level CEFR assignment: https://accenthero.com/app/pronunciation-practice/english/british/treasury and https://mail.accenthero.com/app/pronunciation-practice/english/american/treasury
- **Level finding:** the exact government-department sense is supported, but C1 is not established by the authoritative dictionary pages inspected. Secondary level signals conflict and are low confidence. Keep on hold.
- **Capitalisation / named-entity risk:** distinguish generic lowercase `treasury` from a particular institution styled `the Treasury` or `Treasury Department`. Do not accidentally turn the headword into a country-specific proper name. Also distinguish the government-department sense from funds, a storehouse, and a collection of valued works.
- **Definition refinement:** “The government department responsible for managing public finances.”
- **Myanmar refinement:** `အစိုးရ၏ ဘဏ္ဍာငွေ၊ အခွန်ဝင်ငွေနှင့် အသုံးစရိတ်တို့ကို စီမံခန့်ခွဲသော ဌာန`.
- **Original example proposal:** “The country's treasury announced a revised public-spending plan.”

### 7. warrant — CEFR1000-C1-0824 (shortlist source index 264)

- **Selected sense:** countable noun, an official legal document authorising an action such as a search or arrest.
- **Sense evidence:** Cambridge gives this document sense separately from the verb “make necessary” and the older noun sense meaning a reason: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/warrant
- **CEFR evidence:** the Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary search result for the noun entry labels the legal topic “Law and justice C1”: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warrant_1. The result snippet exposed the topic tag and legal-document sense, but direct page opening returned HTTP 403 on this observation date. A reviewer should verify the page and interpret the topic tag as a domain/topic indicator, not automatically as a global headword-level label.
- **Level finding:** the C1 legal-domain signal is plausible for this exact noun sense, but the source tag needs human confirmation and should not be generalised to all senses or the verb. Keep pending.
- **Myanmar refinement:** `ရှာဖွေခြင်း သို့မဟုတ် ဖမ်းဆီးခြင်းကို ခွင့်ပြုသည့် တရားဝင်ဝရမ်း`.
- **Original example proposal:** “Investigators showed the building manager the warrant before beginning the search.” This avoids copying the dictionary's short “search warrant” or “warrant to search” examples.
