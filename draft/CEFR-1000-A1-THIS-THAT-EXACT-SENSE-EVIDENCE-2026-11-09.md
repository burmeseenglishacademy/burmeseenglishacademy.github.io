## Publisher evidence and exact-sense boundaries
1. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, **this**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this — A1 determiner senses for nearby referents, already-mentioned referents and current/upcoming time. The provisional nearby demonstrative sense is supported, but the publisher also has other A1 senses that should not be silently conflated.
2. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, **that**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/that — A1 determiner “NOT NEAR” and “ALREADY DISCUSSED/SEEN”; separately labels demonstrative pronoun uses. The provisional distant-reference sense is supported.
3. Cambridge Essential British English, **that**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/that — A1 demonstrative determiner/pronoun, while some discourse-linking uses have different level labels. Do not treat every use of `that` as A1.
4. British Council LearnEnglish, **Demonstratives**: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/demonstratives — beginner-level contrast between this/these and that/those; both determiners with nouns and pronouns without nouns. Beginner grammar guidance corroborates teaching context but is **not** independent lexical-sense signoff.
5. British Council LearnEnglish, **Specific and general determiners**: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/specific-general-determiners — classifies this/that as demonstrative determiners.

All URLs examined 2026-11-09. Publisher lexical labels are evidence for the **specified determiner senses**, not for the provisional Myanmar translation or full example quality.

## Disambiguation and independent review tasks
- Keep **determiner + singular noun** distinct from pronoun `This is ...` / `That's ...`, relative `that`, and conjunction `that`. Do not transfer the determiner A1 evidence to these other uses.
- Nearby/far is a useful beginner contrast, **not an exhaustive meaning**: `this` can refer to current time or prior discourse, `that` to shared/prior discourse. Decide whether a separate sense needs another inventory unit, without changing the 200-per-level contract.
- Independent English reviewer: verify exact publisher section and A1 label with dated evidence; review whether the narrower definitions and examples instantiate the selected senses.
- Independent Myanmar reviewer: verify grammar, register, deictic contrast and example translations. Independent pronunciation reviewer: verify UK /ðɪs/ and /ðæt/ and contextual audio.
