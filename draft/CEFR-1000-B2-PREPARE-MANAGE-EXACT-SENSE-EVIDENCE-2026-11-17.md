## B2 provisional: prepare (verb)
- Global shortlist zero-based index 610; original source index 60. Current definition: "To make someone or something ready for an activity." Myanmar: `ပြင်ဆင်သည်`. Existing example: "We need to prepare the documents before the meeting."
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/prepare (observed 2026-11-17), explicitly labels the general **get ready** verb sense **A2** and food-preparation sense **A2**; it separately labels reflexive `prepare yourself` for a difficult situation **B1**.
- Cambridge Essential British English, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/essential-british-english/prepare (observed 2026-11-17), corroborates **A2** for preparing something for the future and **B1** for `prepare yourself` for a difficult situation.
- **Exact-sense conflict:** current B2 definition and document-preparation example use the ordinary A2 "get ready" sense, not the distinct B1 reflexive sense. No cited source substantiates B2 for this exact sense.
- Suggested English: "To make something ready to be used or done." Suggested Myanmar: `အသုံးပြုရန် သို့မဟုတ် လုပ်ဆောင်ရန် အသင့်ဖြစ်အောင် ပြင်ဆင်သည်`. Original example proposal: "Mya prepared the meeting notes before her colleagues arrived." Grammar: transitive past-tense verb; "prepared" is a regular inflection of "prepare", not a new headword. These are editorial candidates only.

## B2 provisional: manage (verb)
- Global shortlist zero-based index 616; original source index 101. Current definition: "To succeed in doing something difficult." Myanmar: `အခက်အခဲရှိသော်လည်း အောင်မြင်စွာ လုပ်နိုင်သည်`. Existing example: "We managed to finish the work before the deadline."
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/manage (observed 2026-11-17), explicitly labels **B1** for "do successfully" and separately **B1** for controlling an office/shop/team. Time/money management and subsistence senses appear separately without a displayed CEFR label in this source.
- Cambridge Essential British English, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/essential-british-english/manage (observed 2026-11-17), independently corroborates **B1** for succeeding at a task and **B1** for being in control of a workplace.
- **Exact-sense conflict:** current B2 definition and `managed to finish` example clearly instantiate Cambridge's **B1** successful-completion sense. A specialized business-management context does not retroactively confer B2 on the successful-completion sense.
- Suggested English: "To succeed in doing something, especially when it is difficult." Suggested Myanmar: `ခက်ခဲသော်လည်း တစ်စုံတစ်ခုကို အောင်မြင်အောင် လုပ်နိုင်သည်`. Original example proposal: "Despite the power cut, the team managed to finish the report." Grammar: `managed to + infinitive`; past tense is a normal inflection, not a lexical mismatch.
