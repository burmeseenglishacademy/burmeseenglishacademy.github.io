## 2. upgrade — C1 provisional; original index 263
- Manifest: verb; “To improve something by replacing it with a newer or better version.” Myanmar: “အဆင့်မြှင့်တင်သည်”. Example: “We will upgrade the software next month.”
- **Contradiction**: Cambridge marks the improve/replace **verb** sense **B2**, and Collins COBUILD marks the equipment/services improvement verb **B2**. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/upgrade and https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/upgrade (observed 2026-11-13). The Cambridge **noun** B2 label is *not* evidence for the verb, but a separate verb B2 label exists.
- Proposed English: “To improve a system or product, often by replacing it with a newer or better version.” Proposed Myanmar: “စနစ် သို့မဟုတ် ပစ္စည်းကို ပိုမိုကောင်းမွန်သော အဆင့်သို့ မြှင့်တင်သည်”. Original proposed example: “The school plans to upgrade its computers before term starts.”
- Review: independent editor to adjudicate the C1 placement, not reinterpret a B2 verb as C1.

## 3. neutral — C1 provisional; original index 155
- Manifest: adjective; “Not supporting either side in a disagreement.” Myanmar: “ဘက်မလိုက်သော”. Example: “The mediator remained neutral during the talks.”
- **Sense-supported**: Cambridge marks the **NO OPINION** adjective sense **C1**. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/neutral (observed 2026-11-13). Do not conflate with neutral colour, chemistry or the noun.
- Proposed English: “Not taking actions or expressing views that support either side in a disagreement.” Proposed Myanmar: “အငြင်းပွားမှုတွင် မည်သည့်ဘက်ကိုမျှ မထောက်ခံဘဲ ဘက်မလိုက်သော”. Original proposed example: “The chairperson stayed neutral while both teams presented their arguments.”
- Review: independent English, fluent Myanmar, example, exact-sense CEFR and provenance signoffs still required; dictionary observation alone is not approval.
