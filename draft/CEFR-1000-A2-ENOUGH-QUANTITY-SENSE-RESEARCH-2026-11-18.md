## External evidence observed 2026-11-18
1. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/enough — explicitly marks pronoun/quantifier NECESSARY sense A2 (“as much as is necessary”), but separately labels WANTED sense B2. Cambridge's part-of-speech label **quantifier**, not specifically determiner, needs alignment to the app's taxonomy.
2. Cambridge English Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/enough — marks the required-amount/degree sense A2 and the more-than-wanted sense B2; includes adverbial use, which must not be silently merged into this determiner record.
3. Cambridge Grammar Today, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/enough — explicitly classifies `enough` as determiner, pronoun or adverb; determiner precedes noun, whereas degree adverb follows adjective/adverb. `enough of` is required before another determiner/pronoun in relevant constructions.
4. British Council quantifiers reference, https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/quantifiers — lists `enough` among quantifiers used with count and uncount nouns, under beginner grammar. This is grammar-teaching evidence, **not** independent exact-sense A2 signoff.
5. British Council “Using 'enough'”, https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/using-enough — covers more complex constructions in B1–B2 grammar; this does not negate Cambridge's explicit A2 classification of the basic necessary-quantity sense.

## Editorial finding and proposed candidates (NOT approvals)
The provisional example is grammatical and clearly illustrates sufficient *number* of plural countable chairs; it does not illustrate uncountable quantity. The definition is broadly accurate for the necessary-quantity sense. Myanmar “လုံလောက်သော” is understandable but alone may imply an adjective; a more instructional candidate is “လိုအပ်သလောက် ပမာဏ သို့မဟုတ် အရေအတွက် ရှိသော (နာမ်ရှေ့တွင် သုံးသည်)”. Natural concise alternative: “လိုအပ်သလောက် လုံလောက်သော”. Independent Myanmar reviewer should select appropriate register and confirm that both countable and uncountable use are covered.

Original example candidates, authored for editorial comparison:
- Plural count noun: “We have enough cups for all the guests.” — “ဧည့်သည်အားလုံးအတွက် ခွက်အလုံအလောက် ရှိပါတယ်။”
- Uncountable noun: “There is enough water for the journey.” — “ခရီးအတွက် ရေအလုံအလောက် ရှိပါတယ်။”
- Contrast (not for this record): “The bag is big enough.” Here `enough` is an **adverb**, not a determiner.
