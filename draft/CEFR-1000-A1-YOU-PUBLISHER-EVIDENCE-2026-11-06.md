## Candidate editorial clarification (not applied to shortlist)

**English meaning candidate:** “The pronoun used to speak directly to one person or more than one person.” **Myanmar meaning candidate:** “မိမိ စကားပြောဆိုနေသူ တစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် တစ်ဦးထက်ပိုသူများကို ရည်ညွှန်းသည့် နာမ်စား။” **Original example candidate:** “You can sit here.” Candidate Myanmar example translation: “ဒီနေရာမှာ ထိုင်လို့ရပါတယ်။” This sentence is a permission example addressing the listener; the English pronoun is the subject. The first-pass example is also grammatical but may be ambiguous about number of addressees.
