## Direct observations (checked 2026-11-14)

1. Cambridge Grammar, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/this-that-these-those — distinguishes **determiner** uses before nouns from **pronoun** uses without nouns; `this`/`that` are singular and can modify singular count or uncountable nouns. **No explicit A1 CEFR assignment** on this grammar page.
2. British Council LearnEnglish, https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/demonstratives — classifies demonstratives as **beginner** grammar and explains near/far reference with nouns. **Beginner is not an independently attested exact-sense A1 label.**
3. British Council LearnEnglish Teens, https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/grammar/a1-a2-grammar/these-those — places the demonstratives lesson in the **A1–A2 grammar** collection and differentiates spatial distance, singular/plural and pronoun/determiner uses. **The collection's range does not independently settle A1 for either individual determiner sense.**

## Existing provisional records and editorial notes

| 7 | this | Used to point to a person or thing near the speaker. | ဒီ၊ ဤ | This bag is mine. |
| 8 | that | Used to point to a person or thing farther away. | ဟို၊ အဲဒီ | That house is big. |

Both examples use **determiners** before singular nouns (`bag`, `house`). Existing definitions do not explicitly say that the target use is **before a noun**, allowing inadvertent mixing with pronoun senses. The Myanmar forms are context/register-sensitive: `ဒီ` is everyday colloquial, `ဤ` formal/written; `ဟို` points more clearly to spatial distance while `အဲဒီ` may be anaphoric or situational. An independent fluent Myanmar editor must decide what is natural in the lesson's register and whether to give contextual sentence translations.

## Proposed unapproved copy for independent adjudication

- **this — demonstrative determiner, near speaker:** “Used before a singular noun to identify a person or thing close to the speaker.” Myanmar candidate: “ပြောသူအနီးရှိ လူ သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းရန် နာမ်ရှေ့တွင် သုံးသော စကားလုံး။” Original example: “This cup is for you.” Example Myanmar candidate: “ဒီခွက်က မင်းအတွက်ပါ။”
- **that — demonstrative determiner, farther from speaker:** “Used before a singular noun to identify a person or thing farther from the speaker.” Myanmar candidate: “ပြောသူနှင့် အလှမ်းဝေးသော လူ သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းရန် နာမ်ရှေ့တွင် သုံးသော စကားလုံး။” Original example: “That bus stops near the school.” Example Myanmar candidate: “ဟိုဘတ်စ်ကားက ကျောင်းနားမှာ ရပ်တယ်။”

**Caveat:** The proposed English wording restricts to singular nouns for beginner clarity, but Cambridge confirms uncountable noun uses too (`this water`, `that juice`). A reviewer should decide whether to include “singular or uncountable noun” rather than accidentally presenting a false exhaustive rule.
