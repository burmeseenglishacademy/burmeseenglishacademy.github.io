## A1 entry: a (article; source index 0)
- **Current English:** “Used before one person or thing when it is not specified.”
- **Current Myanmar:** “တစ်ခု၊ တစ်ယောက် (မသတ်မှတ်ရသေးသော)”
- **Current example:** “I have a book.”
- **Observed evidence:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary explicitly labels the indefinite determiner before-noun sense **A1** (non-specific single person/thing). It separately labels some other uses of *a* A2. URL: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/a (observed 2026-11-13). British Council corroborates the indefinite article's use with singular count nouns: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/indefinite-article (observed 2026-11-13).
- **Editorial candidate (not applied):** “An article used before a singular countable noun when the person or thing is not identified.” Myanmar candidate: “တိတိကျကျ မဖော်ပြထားသော တစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် တစ်ခုကို ရည်ညွှန်းသည့် ရေတွက်နိုင်သော အနည်းကိန်းနာမ်ရှေ့တွင် သုံးသော စကားလုံး။” Original learner example remains grammatical. Independent Myanmar fluency and teaching suitability review needed.

## A1 entry: an (article; source index 1)
- **Current English:** “Used before one person or thing when the next word starts with a vowel sound.”
- **Current Myanmar:** “တစ်ခု၊ တစ်ယောက် (သရသံဖြင့်စသော စကားလုံးရှေ့တွင်)”
- **Current example:** “She has an apple.”
- **Observed evidence:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary explicitly labels the determiner *an* **A1**, used in place of *a* before a following vowel **sound**, not necessarily a vowel letter. URL: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/an (observed 2026-11-13). Cambridge British Grammar clarifies contrastive cases *a university* and *an hour*: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/a-an-and-the (observed 2026-11-13).
- **Editorial candidate (not applied):** “The form of the indefinite article used before a vowel sound.” Myanmar candidate: “နောက်ကလိုက်လာသော စကားလုံး၏ အစသံသည် သရသံဖြစ်သည့်အခါ ‘a’ အစား သုံးသော မသတ်မှတ်ပြ စကားလုံး။” Original learner example remains grammatical. Independent Myanmar fluency and teaching suitability review needed.
