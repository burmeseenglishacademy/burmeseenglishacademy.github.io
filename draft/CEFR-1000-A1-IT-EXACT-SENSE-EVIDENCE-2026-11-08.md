## Editorial recommendation (not yet applied)
Target **referential pronoun**, not weather/time dummy subject or capitalised `IT` meaning information technology (Cambridge labels that abbreviation A2). Proposed precise English definition: **A pronoun used instead of the name of a thing or animal that has already been mentioned.** Suggested fluent Myanmar gloss: **အရင်က ပြောထားပြီးသား ပစ္စည်း သို့မဟုတ် တိရစ္ဆာန်ကို နာမည်ထပ်မပြောဘဲ အစားထိုးရည်ညွှန်းတဲ့ နာမ်စား။** This is a proposed teaching paraphrase, not a publisher translation.

Original example (subject): **I have a blue bag. It is new.** / **ကျွန်တော့်မှာ အပြာရောင်အိတ်တစ်လုံးရှိတယ်။ အဲဒီအိတ်က အသစ်ပါ။**

Original example (object): **Where is my book? I cannot find it.** / **ကျွန်တော့်စာအုပ် ဘယ်မှာလဲ။ အဲဒီစာအုပ်ကို ရှာမတွေ့ဘူး။**

The explicit antecedents **bag** and **book** avoid confusing the referential A1 sense with dummy `it`. Verify register and Myanmar pronoun choice independently. Grammar checks: singular reference; `it is` vs `its` vs `it's`; subject/object positions; do not use this entry to teach `It is raining` as if it referred to a previously mentioned object.
