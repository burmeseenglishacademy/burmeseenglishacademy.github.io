## Exact-sense decision needed

The existing definition conflates copular identity/quality with bare lexical “exist”. The current example (“I want to be a teacher”) directly tests **copular identity/profession**, not existential `there is` and not literary standalone `be` = exist. Recommend specifying the A1 entry as **linking verb: describe identity, job, state or quality**. Preserve existential/locative and auxiliary uses as distinct review candidates, not silently combined senses. The Cambridge Learner's Dictionary explicitly separates A1 linking and A2 auxiliary senses; **headword-level A1 does not license every use of be as A1**.
