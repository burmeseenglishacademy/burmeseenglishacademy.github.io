## Evidence inspected (original source URLs)
1. Cambridge Dictionary, *As, when or while?*: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/as-when-or-while — conjunctions introduce clauses; temporal `while` connects simultaneous activities; placement before/after main clause; distinguishes `when` for interrupting events.
2. Cambridge Dictionary, *Conjunctions: time*: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/while — `while` connects simultaneous events; `during` is a preposition and cannot directly introduce a finite clause.
3. British Council LearnEnglish, *Contrasting ideas* (C1 grammar resource): https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/contrasting-ideas — contrastive/concessive `while` is taught as part of C1 contrasting ideas; this is **not proof** that every contrastive `while` lexical sense is C1 or that temporal `while` is A2.
4. Cambridge Dictionary, *Whereas*: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whereas — `whereas` can substitute for contrastive `while` but not temporal `while`.

## Editorial assessment
The current A2 example matches the **temporal** conjunction meaning and is grammatical: two events overlap. The Myanmar gloss “နေစဉ်” is contextually usable but should be embedded in a clear clause-based explanation. “အချိန်အတွင်း” alone can be mistaken for a prepositional `during + noun` construction; it does not adequately explain the subordinate-clause grammar.

**Candidate exact-sense English definition (unapproved):** “Used to connect two actions or situations happening at the same time.”

**Candidate Myanmar explanation (unapproved):** “အဖြစ်အပျက်နှစ်ခု တစ်ချိန်တည်း ဖြစ်ပျက်နေကြောင်း ပြောရာတွင် ဝါကျခွဲနှစ်ခုကို ဆက်ပေးသော စကားလုံး။ ‘…နေစဉ်’၊ ‘…နေတုန်း’ ဟု အခြေအနေအလိုက် ပြန်ဆိုနိုင်သည်။”

**Original example (unapproved):** “While my sister was reading, I made some tea.”  
**Original Myanmar example translation (unapproved):** “အစ်မ စာဖတ်နေတုန်း ကျွန်တော် လက်ဖက်ရည် ဖျော်ခဲ့တယ်။” (Speaker-specific first-person register; a gender-neutral teaching example may be preferable.)

**Disambiguation for independent reviewers:** Temporal: “I took notes while the teacher explained the task.” Contrastive: “While the first route is shorter, the second is safer.” The latter does **not** mean the two routes are happening simultaneously. Do not silently combine these senses under one A2 entry or infer a CEFR label from a grammar-course page.
