## `be` — A1, verb
Existing definition: “To exist or to have a particular quality or identity.” Existing Myanmar: “ဖြစ်သည်၊ ရှိသည်”. Existing example: “I want to be a teacher.”

Evidence:
- Cambridge English Dictionary, `be`, DESCRIPTION sense: explicitly A1 for a linking verb describing quality, state, identity or job, with a closely matching job-aspiration example. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/be
- Collins English Dictionary, `be`, linking-verb sense: explicitly A1 for identity, quality and position. https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/be

Editorial finding: the original example is grammatical and supports the job/identity reading, but “to exist” is an additional reading not demonstrated by this example; do not treat every sense of `be` as proven A1 by the cited linking-verb evidence. The original Myanmar gloss is broad, potentially collapsing linking `be` and existential `be`.

Unapproved proposed teaching definition: “Used to say what someone or something is, or what they are like.” Proposed Myanmar: “တစ်ဦးဦး သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခု၏ အမျိုးအစား၊ အခြေအနေ၊ အရည်အသွေးကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် သုံးသည်။” Proposed original example: “My sister is a teacher.” If retaining the infinitive headword in the example, the existing “I want to be a teacher.” remains grammatically valid. Independently adjudicate gloss naturalness and whether the intended lexical sense is the linking verb versus existential use.

## `we` — A1, pronoun
Existing definition: “The speaker and one or more other people.” Existing Myanmar: “ကျွန်ုပ်တို့၊ ကျွန်တော်တို့”. Existing example: “We go to school.”

Evidence:
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, `we`: explicitly A1 for the speaker plus other person(s), used as a verb subject; separately B1 for people in general. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/learner-english/we
- Cambridge English Dictionary, `we` GROUP: A1; ALL PEOPLE: B1; additional special usages must not be folded into the A1 sense. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/we
- Collins English Dictionary, `we`: A1 for speaker plus others, B1 for people in general. https://www.collinsdictionary.com/zh/dictionary/english/we

Editorial finding: original example is grammatical but does not make the referent group explicit. Myanmar pronoun choice is register-sensitive: `ကျွန်ုပ်တို့` is comparatively formal/written; `ကျွန်တော်တို့` is ordinary polite first-person plural but not universally suitable for all speaker identities and situations. Avoid claiming a one-to-one register-neutral Myanmar equivalent. English `we` may include or exclude the addressee, as context determines.

Unapproved proposed definition: “Used as the subject when the speaker means themselves and at least one other person.” Proposed Myanmar teaching note: “ပြောသူကိုယ်တိုင်နှင့် အခြားသူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် အများပါဝင်သော အုပ်စုကို ရည်ညွှန်းသည့် နာမ်စား (ဥပမာ — ကျွန်တော်တို့၊ ကျွန်မတို့၊ ကျွန်ုပ်တို့)။” Proposed original example: “My brother and I are ready. We can leave now.” This explicitly establishes the referent and the subject function. Independent Myanmar fluency and register review required.
