## Specific fail-open risks found in the current Node readiness checker

1. **Self-attested independence:** `independent_*_review: true`, `reviewer_id` and `reviewer_signed_at` are accepted as strings/booleans in a JSON manifest. A fabricated or author-authored manifest could satisfy these syntactic checks. Require independent reviewer signoff bound to a content hash and an externally auditable identity/decision record; independently verify that the reviewer is not the content author. Do not infer authenticity from GitHub commit authorship alone.
2. **Source-backed exact-sense CEFR:** `source_url` only has an HTTPS format check; the gate does not fetch or establish that the cited page supports the exact definition, part of speech, and assigned CEFR level. Human reviewer must record quoted sense locator, publisher, observation date, and a reasoned match; disagreements stay on hold.
3. **Audio listening:** `listened: true` and `listener_id` are self-reported. File existence, nonzero length and SHA-256 integrity do not prove British voice identity, correct pronunciation, audio quality or actual listening. Require independently signed listening sheets tied to all four per-task audio hashes and device/playback context; validate real samples and exceptions.
4. **Mobile and physical iPhone:** `physical_iphone_safari.passed` plus an HTTPS evidence URL is not physical-device proof. Preserve device model, iOS/Safari version, test date, screen recordings or screenshots, reproducible checklist, tester identity and independent attestation.
5. **Production claim:** `production_deployment.passed` and a `production_commit_sha` are only claims. A release authority must independently compare the actual public GitHub Pages deployment SHA/content, URL, asset hashes and live smoke tests after the protected-production snapshot.
6. **Audio-path constraints:** the current gate accepts arbitrary files under `draft/` with a matching checksum; it does not validate that they are playable audio, correctly named British George/Emma voices, or that each audio actually speaks the intended headword/example. Require media decode, duration/format checks and listening review.
7. **Date and URL semantics:** nonempty timestamps and HTTPS strings are not proof of an observation, source retention or identity. Record archival snapshots or immutable evidence references and independently verify access.

## Mandatory reviewer record (one per original task ID)

Record the immutable source shortlist blob SHA, task ID, original source path/index/blob, proposed headword/POS/exact sense, English definition, idiomatic Myanmar meaning, independently checked original example, source publisher and exact sense locator, CEFR level evidence URL and observation date, reviewer identity and independent signature/date, editorial conflict resolution, and a decision of APPROVE/REVISE/REJECT. A revision requires fresh independent signoff. Reviewer signoff must never be generated by the same process that authors the draft.
