## Proposed exact-sense restriction — needs external editorial verification
- **Target sense:** `be`, linking verb in simple present, connecting a person or thing to identity, condition or location (`am/is/are`). Keep auxiliary progressive/passive, modal constructions, existential `there is`, and advanced uses outside this single sense until separately evidenced.
- **English learner definition proposal:** “Used to say who or what someone or something is, how they are, or where they are.”
- **Myanmar learner explanation proposal:** “လူတစ်ယောက် သို့မဟုတ် အရာဝတ္ထုတစ်ခုက ဘယ်သူ၊ ဘာဖြစ်တယ်၊ ဘယ်လိုအခြေအနေရှိတယ်၊ ဘယ်နေရာမှာရှိတယ်ဆိုတာ ပြောရာမှာ သုံးတဲ့ ကြိယာ။ ပစ္စုပ္ပန်ကာလမှာ am, is, are လို့ ပြောင်းသုံးရတယ်။”
- **Original example proposal:** “My sister is at home.” / “ကျွန်တော့်အစ်မက အိမ်မှာ ရှိတယ်။” (Alternative speaker-neutral: “သူ့အစ်မက အိမ်မှာ ရှိတယ်။”)
- **Additional original contrast:** “She is a nurse.” / “သူက သူနာပြုတစ်ယောက်ပါ။” Here Burmese often uses `ပါ` or zero copula rather than translating every `is` as `ဖြစ်သည်`.
- **Grammar check:** `sister` singular third-person -> `is`; `at home` location complement. Do not present the English base form `be` as the surface verb in this example.
