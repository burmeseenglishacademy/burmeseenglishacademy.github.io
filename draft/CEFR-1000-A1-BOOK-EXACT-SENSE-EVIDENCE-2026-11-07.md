## Proposed editorial improvements (not applied to inventory)

- Exact sense: **book (countable noun)** — a set of printed or written pages joined inside a cover, intended for reading.
- Fluent Myanmar core gloss: **စာအုပ်**. For explanatory teaching only: **ဖတ်ရန် စာမျက်နှာများကို စုစည်းချုပ်ထားသော အရာ**. Avoid translating the verb “to book” as “စာအုပ်”.
- Original example option: **I borrowed a book from the library.** Myanmar: **စာကြည့်တိုက်ကနေ စာအုပ်တစ်အုပ် ငှားခဲ့တယ်။** This is an editorial proposal, not a reviewed replacement. The original “This book is interesting.” is grammatically valid and need not be changed merely for novelty.
- Pronunciation: British **/bʊk/**, one syllable, short /ʊ/ vowel; check both George and Emma headword and sentence recordings after generation. Do not infer listening success from IPA.
- Disambiguation: **book** noun A1 ≠ **book** verb A2 (“ကြိုတင်မှာယူသည်/ကြိုတင်ဘွတ်ကင်လုပ်သည်” in reservation contexts); **books** as accounting records is a separate sense. The current shortlist contains the A1 noun entry; do not add a verb or change counts without editorial rebalancing.
