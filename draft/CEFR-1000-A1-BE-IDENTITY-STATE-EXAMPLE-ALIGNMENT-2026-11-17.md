## Exact-use problem
The provisional English definition says: “To exist or to have a particular quality or identity.” Its original example is “I want to be a teacher.” The example illustrates **identity in an infinitival complement** (`want + to + be + noun phrase`), not an unambiguous independent illustration of *existence*. Avoid presenting the two senses as equally evidenced by that single example. A learner may also confuse lexical `be` with auxiliary `be` in progressive or passive constructions. The British Council beginner lesson establishes *present-simple identity/state* as a teachable construction, but the example in the provisional record uses the infinitive. This alignment should be reviewed explicitly.
