## this — A1 demonstrative determiner, near speaker
Publisher exact-sense evidence:
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, observed 2026-11-06: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this — `this determiner (NEAR)` explicitly A1; also A1 `ALREADY TALKED ABOUT` and `TIME`, which are distinct usage targets.
- Cambridge English Dictionary, observed 2026-11-06: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/this — determiner/pronoun pointing to an identifiable referent A1; broad entry, less precise than Learner's NEAR label.

Candidate original English: 'Used before a singular noun to point to a person or thing near the speaker.' Candidate Myanmar: 'ပြောသူနှင့် နီးနေသော လူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းရာတွင် နာမ်ရှေ့၌ သုံးသော စကားလုံး။' Candidate original English example: 'This chair beside me is comfortable.' Candidate Myanmar example: 'ကျွန်တော့်ဘေးက ဒီကုလားထိုင်က ထိုင်လို့ သက်သောင့်သက်သာရှိတယ်။'

Reviewer checks: singular noun/determiner (not independent pronoun); `these` is plural, not another meaning of `this`; `this year` is a separate temporal A1 sense; `this` for previously discussed information is separately classified; verify Myanmar demonstrative register and naturalness; example must demonstrate physical proximity, not merely identify an object.

## that — A1 demonstrative determiner, not near speaker
Publisher exact-sense evidence:
- Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, observed 2026-11-06: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/that — `that determiner (NOT NEAR)` explicitly A1; `ALREADY DISCUSSED/SEEN` also A1 but distinct.
- Cambridge English Dictionary, observed 2026-11-06: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/that — determiner distant in space/time A1; previously mentioned referent A1; `that` as conjunction A2, relative pronoun B1, degree adverb B2: DO NOT conflate these with the A1 demonstrative determiner.

Candidate original English: 'Used before a singular noun to point to a person or thing away from the speaker.' Candidate Myanmar: 'ပြောသူနှင့် အလှမ်းဝေးနေသော လူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းရာတွင် နာမ်ရှေ့၌ သုံးသော စကားလုံး။' Candidate original English example: 'That tree across the road is very tall.' Candidate Myanmar example: 'လမ်းတစ်ဖက်က ဟိုသစ်ပင်က အရမ်းမြင့်တယ်။'

Reviewer checks: `that tree` is determiner+noun, whereas `That is tall` is pronoun; `those` is plural; distinguish physical distance from anaphoric or time distance; ensure Burmese `ဟို` versus `အဲဒီ` matches intended distance and discourse context.
