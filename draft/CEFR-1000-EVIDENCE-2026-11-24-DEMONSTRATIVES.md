## this — source index 7
Source: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this
Observed determiner NEAR sense explicitly A1; UK /ðɪs/. Proposed definition: Points to a nearby person or thing before a noun. Myanmar: ဒီ၊ ဤ (အနီးရှိ လူ သို့မဟုတ် အရာကို ညွှန်းရာတွင် သုံးသည်)။ Existing example “This bag is mine.” is grammatical, with this modifying singular bag; proposed translation ဒီအိတ်က ကျွန်တော့်အိတ်ပါ။ The source separately labels previously discussed and time senses A1; do not conflate them with this spatial sense.

## that — source index 8
Source: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/that
