## Source observations (retrieved 2026-11-20)

1. Cambridge Dictionary, **finally**, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/finally : labels the **AFTER TIME** adverb sense **A2**; labels the **LAST** discourse-organiser sense **B1**. The candidate A2 definition and bus-stop example correspond to AFTER TIME, *not* the last-point-in-a-list sense.
2. Cambridge Dictionary, **eventually**, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/eventually : labels the delayed-outcome adverb **B2**, rather than the candidate B1. The candidate definition and attempts/example strongly align with that delayed-outcome sense. **Substantive unresolved B1→B2 placement conflict; independent source-backed adjudication required.**
3. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, **eventually**, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/eventually : independently displayed Cambridge learner entry also labels this delayed-outcome sense **B2** (a second Cambridge page, **not** an independent reviewer).
4. Cambridge Grammar Today, **Eventually**, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/eventually : notes that eventually describes a result after time/effort, **not** “possibly/if possible”; also distinguishes list-final `finally` from `eventually`.

These are live source observations, not frozen copies or evidence of reviewer independence. Dictionary labels are source-specific and should not be silently treated as a universal CEFR ruling.
