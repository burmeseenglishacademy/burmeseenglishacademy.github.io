## 1. signature — C1 classification conflicts with exact-sense B1 evidence

Current: noun; "A person's name written in their own handwriting." Myanmar `လက်မှတ်ရေးထိုးချက်`; example "Please add your signature at the bottom of the form."

**Observed exact-sense evidence:** Cambridge Learner's Dictionary labels the handwritten-name noun `signature` **B1** (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/signature). Cambridge Essential British English also labels that same noun sense **B1** (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/signature). Both consulted 2026-11-18. These are direct evidence against treating the current ordinary handwritten-name sense as C1 *under Cambridge's scale*. Other senses, such as a distinctive identifying feature, must not be substituted silently to justify C1.

Myanmar: `စာရွက်စာတမ်းတစ်ခုကို အတည်ပြုရန် သို့မဟုတ် သဘောတူကြောင်း ပြသရန် ကိုယ်တိုင်ရေးထိုးထားသော နာမည် (လက်မှတ်)`. Original example: "The customer put her signature on the delivery form." Myanmar example: `ဖောက်သည်က ပစ္စည်းပို့ဆောင်မှုဖောင်ပေါ်မှာ လက်မှတ်ရေးထိုးခဲ့သည်။` The current English example is grammatical, but its level cannot be inferred from grammar alone.

## 2. neutral — C1 exact-sense evidence aligns, but review remains open

Current: adjective; "Not supporting either side in a disagreement." Myanmar `ဘက်မလိုက်သော`; example "The mediator remained neutral during the talks."

**Observed exact-sense evidence:** Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary marks the adjective `neutral` in the **NO OPINION** sense **C1** (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/neutral), consulted 2026-11-18. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/neutral) describes independent/non-supporting behaviour but does not show a CEFR level in the retrieved entry. Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary (https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/neutral_1) describes the disagreement/contest sense without an observed explicit C1 label. The Cambridge C1 label matches the current sense; this is preliminary source evidence, not independent signoff.

**Candidate only, not applied:** English: "Not supporting or helping either side in a disagreement or conflict." Myanmar: `အငြင်းပွားမှု သို့မဟုတ် ပဋိပက္ခတစ်ခုတွင် ဘယ်ဘက်ကိုမှ မထောက်ခံဘဲ ဘက်မလိုက်သော`. Original example: "The chairperson stayed neutral while both teams presented their arguments." Myanmar example: `အဖွဲ့နှစ်ဖွဲ့စလုံးက မိမိတို့အကြောင်းပြချက်တွေကို တင်ပြနေစဉ် ဥက္ကဋ္ဌက ဘက်မလိုက်ဘဲ နေခဲ့သည်။` The existing mediator example is grammatical and matches the C1 NO OPINION sense.
