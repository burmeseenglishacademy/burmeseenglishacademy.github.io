## Source observations (publisher-backed)

1. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, [a](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/a), observed 2026-11-06. The **a** determiner entry labels its *BEFORE NOUN* sense **A1**, and explicitly groups **an** as its alternative form. Other senses under the same entry have distinct A1 and A2 labels. Thus a general headword-level CEFR assignment is insufficient: retain the sense label and its grammatical function.
2. Cambridge English Grammar Today, [A/an and the](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/a-and-the), observed 2026-11-06. Article selection depends on the **sound immediately following** the article, not simply the initial written letter. This page documents vowel-letter/consonant-sound and consonant-letter/vowel-sound counterexamples. It is grammar support, **not** an independent CEFR rating.
3. Collins COBUILD, [a](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/a), observed 2026-11-06, separately labels the indefinite-article determiner sense **A1**, treats **a/an** together, and explains the vowel-sound condition. Secondary publisher corroboration; check exact sense boundaries before approval.
