# CEFR-1000 A1 `home`: noun/adverb exact-sense mismatch — editorial research (2026-11-08)

**Status:** research proposal only; editorial hold remains; no independent review or CEFR signoff. **Scope:** user-approved 1,000 total, not historical 5,000.

## Inventory provenance
- Shortlist: `draft/CEFR-1000-PROVISIONAL-SHORTLIST.json`, Git blob `475e68ae17ab97bd937e73e5ce4794e0dcd76551`, entry `home`, A1, `source_path=draft/A1-EDITORIAL-STARTER.json`, `source_index=22`, `source_blob_sha=ff1e93f60828ef2f94c9146522ce94075183b0ae`.
- Current POS: **noun**. Current definition: “The place where someone lives.” Current Myanmar: “အိမ်၊ နေအိမ်”. Current example: “I go home after school.”
- **Problem:** `home` in `go home` functions as a directional **adverb**, not the inventory's noun sense. The noun definition and example therefore do not demonstrate the same POS/sense. This is not proof that the word is wrongly levelled; it is a concrete example-to-sense mismatch.

## Publisher observations (accessed 2026-11-08)
1. Cambridge Dictionary, [home](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/home): **home noun (HOUSE/APARTMENT)** explicitly **A1**; UK pronunciation **/həʊm/**. A separate **home noun (ORIGIN)** sense is also A1; care-home institution sense is C1. Do not transfer the A1 label indiscriminately across senses.
2. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, [home](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/home): noun meaning place where someone lives or belongs is explicitly **A1**; adverbial directional uses such as `go home` appear separately. Do not treat the noun's A1 tag as independent evidence for the adverb's level.
3. Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary, [home noun](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/home_1): independently corroborates noun POS and UK /həʊm/ pronunciation; the page is not relied on for an explicit CEFR label.
4. British Council LearnEnglish, [House or home?](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/learning-hub/discussions/4-house-or-home): explicitly contrasts **house** (building) with **home** (place of living/belonging) and notes adverbial `come home` / `stay home`. This is grammar support, not a publisher's A1 classification for the adverb.
