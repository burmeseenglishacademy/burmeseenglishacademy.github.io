## this — exact determiner senses

Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/this (observed 2026-11-19), explicitly labels **A1** the determiner senses (i) pointing to a nearby person/thing, (ii) referencing a previously mentioned thing, and (iii) the current or upcoming time period. These are separate senses; the provisional example illustrates (i), not necessarily all three.

**Proposed narrow A1 learner definition (editorial draft):** Used before a noun to identify a person or thing close to the speaker or being pointed out.

**Proposed Myanmar learner meaning:** ပြောသူနားမှာရှိတဲ့၊ ဒါမှမဟုတ် လက်ညှိုးထိုးပြနေတဲ့ လူတစ်ယောက် သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းတဲ့အခါ နာမ်ရှေ့မှာ သုံးတဲ့စကား။ “ဒီ…”၊ “ဒီဟာ…” ဆိုတဲ့ အဓိပ္ပာယ်မျိုးရှိတယ်။

**New original example:** “This chair is for my grandmother.”

**Myanmar example:** “ဒီကုလားထိုင်က ကျွန်တော့်အဘွားအတွက်ပါ။” (Speaker-register variant: “ကျွန်မ့အဘွားအတွက်ပါ။”)

**Additional sense to keep distinct:** “I am busy this week.” = “ဒီအပတ် ကျွန်တော် အလုပ်များနေတယ်။” Cambridge labels the time-period determiner A1, but it must not be conflated with spatial proximity in a single exact-sense signoff.

## that — exact determiner senses

Cambridge Essential British English Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/essential-british-english/that (observed 2026-11-19), explicitly labels **A1** both the determiner pointing to someone/something not near the speaker and the determiner referring to something already seen or discussed. Cambridge Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/that (observed 2026-11-19), independently presents these *lexicographic* senses with A1 labels; two dictionary entries are not two independent project reviewers. Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/that (observed 2026-11-19), also distinguishes an **A2 conjunction** use; that grammatical use is outside this provisional determiner record.

**Proposed narrow A1 learner definition (editorial draft):** Used before a noun to point to a person or thing farther from the speaker.

**Proposed Myanmar learner meaning:** ပြောသူနဲ့ အနည်းငယ်ဝေးတဲ့ လူတစ်ယောက် ဒါမှမဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခုကို ညွှန်းတဲ့အခါ နာမ်ရှေ့မှာ သုံးတဲ့စကား။ အခြေအနေအလိုက် “ဟို…”၊ “အဲဒီ…” လို့ ပြောနိုင်တယ်။

**New original example:** “That blue bicycle belongs to my brother.”

**Myanmar example:** “ဟိုအပြာရောင်စက်ဘီးက ကျွန်တော့်အစ်ကိုရဲ့ စက်ဘီးပါ။” (Speaker-register variant: “ကျွန်မ့အစ်ကိုရဲ့ စက်ဘီးပါ။”)

**Additional sense to keep distinct:** “Please bring that book we discussed.” = “ငါတို့ ပြောခဲ့တဲ့ အဲဒီစာအုပ်ကို ယူလာပေးပါ။” This refers to previously discussed information, not necessarily physical distance; register of “ငါတို့” should be checked for the learner audience.

## Cross-entry editorial risks and independent-review instructions

1. Do not treat “this = near” / “that = far” as exhaustive: discourse and time-reference uses are separately documented; examples must align to the chosen sense.

3. The contrast is relative to speaker/context, not a fixed distance threshold. Myanmar “ဒီ/အဲဒီ/ဟို” choices depend on shared context and discourse as well as distance; native-fluent independent review is required.
