### A2 index 230: however (contrast discourse adverb)
- Existing definition: "Used to introduce a fact that contrasts with what was just said."
- Existing Myanmar: "သို့သော်၊ ဒါပေမဲ့"
- Existing example: "The room was small; however, it was comfortable."
- Cambridge explicitly labels the **DESPITE THIS** adverb sense **A2**, whereas **DEGREE** ("however much") is B2 and **WAY** ("however you like") is C2. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/however
- Collins independently displays **A2** for contrast and **B2** for the degree sense. https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/however
- Finding: provisional A2 aligns with the precise contrast sense, but a bare-word CEFR match would be unsafe; preserve sense key in final manifest. The example's semicolon + conjunctive adverb + comma is grammatical. Proposed optional simpler A2 example (original editorial proposal): "The bus was late. However, we arrived on time." Myanmar gloss can remain; note that ဒါပေမဲ့ is more conversational than formal သို့သော်.
- Independent reviewer must confirm exact sense, register, Myanmar idiomaticity and example before signoff.

### C1 index 915: sustainable (environmental adjective)
- Existing definition: "Able to continue without exhausting resources or causing serious harm."
- Existing Myanmar: "သယံဇာတများ မကုန်ခန်းစေဘဲ ရေရှည်ဆက်လက်လုပ်ဆောင်နိုင်သော"
- Existing example: "The company is developing a sustainable packaging strategy."
- Cambridge marks both general "able to continue over a period of time" and the **environment-specialized** low-harm sense **C1**. https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sustainable
- Finding: provisional C1 is source-supported for the environment-specialized sense. Current definition merges general continuity and environmental stewardship; final definition should distinguish these. Proposed unapproved environmental definition: "Designed to use resources responsibly and avoid long-term environmental damage." Proposed concise Myanmar gloss: "သဘာဝပတ်ဝန်းကျင်ကို မထိခိုက်စေဘဲ ရေရှည်တည်တံ့နိုင်သော". Original proposed example: "The factory is switching to sustainable packaging materials."
- The existing "strategy" example could be interpreted broadly as business continuity, not necessarily environmental impact. Require reviewer to confirm which sense is actually exemplified.
- Independent reviewer must check translation nuance, example sense, CEFR, and any new source evidence before signoff.
