### 1. prepare — B2 / verb — task CEFR1000-B2-0611
- Original source index: **60**.
- Existing English definition: “To make someone or something ready for an activity.”
- Existing Myanmar: **ပြင်ဆင်သည်**.
- Existing original example: “We need to prepare the documents before the meeting.”
- **Observed external evidence:** Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary marks the *make/get ready* sense **A2**, and a different *expect and be ready* sense **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/prepare . Cambridge Learner's Dictionary marks *get ready* **A2** and *prepare yourself* for difficulty **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/prepare . Cambridge Essential British English independently within the publisher's dictionary family marks *get ready* **A2**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/prepare .

Example grammar is sound; complexity of the workplace context does not elevate the verb sense to B2. Myanmar **ပြင်ဆင်သည်** is plausible for document preparation but requires fluent Myanmar independent review for context and register.

Consider reassignment or replacement **only** with independently substantiated CEFR evidence and inventory-preserving edits. Do not relabel the existing A2 sense as B2 based on the example context.

### 2. calm — B2 / adjective — task CEFR1000-B2-0612
- Original source index: **65**.
- Existing English definition: “Not worried, excited or upset.”
- Existing Myanmar: **တည်ငြိမ်သော**.
- Existing original example: “She remained calm during the emergency.”
- **Observed external evidence:** Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary labels the *peaceful/quiet/without worry* adjective sense **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/calm . Cambridge Learner's Dictionary separately labels its *person relaxed/not worried* adjective sense **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/calm . Cambridge Essential British English also marks the corresponding adjective sense **B1**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/essential-british-english/calm .
- **Sense assessment (author, unapproved):** “remained calm during the emergency” uses the B1 *not worried/upset* sense; the emergency setting does not itself justify B2. The example is grammatical. Myanmar **တည်ငြိမ်သော** conveys steadiness, but independent fluent review should test whether **စိတ်တည်ငြိမ်သော** or **စိတ်အေးအေးထားနိုင်သော** more clearly conveys emotional composure in this context; these are **candidates only**, not approved translations.
- **Reviewer action:** Independently verify English exact sense, CEFR, Myanmar naturalness, example and British audio pronunciation. Do not claim B2 support from a dictionary's B1 label.
