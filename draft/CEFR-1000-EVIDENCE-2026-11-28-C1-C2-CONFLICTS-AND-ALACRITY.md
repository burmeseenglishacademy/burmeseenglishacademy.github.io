### 5. alacrity — noun

- **Task / origin:** `CEFR1000-C1-1000`; batch59, source index 29. This is the entry already quarantined in the historical batch59 record for unsupported C1/C2 placement.
- **Shortlist sense:** cheerful willingness and speed in doing something.
- **Dictionary sense:** Cambridge defines it as speed and eagerness; UK pronunciation `/əˈlæk.rə.ti/`. Oxford’s Advanced Learner’s Dictionary entry gives “great happiness or enthusiasm” and explicitly displays **From the Word list: Oxford 3000 — A2**.
