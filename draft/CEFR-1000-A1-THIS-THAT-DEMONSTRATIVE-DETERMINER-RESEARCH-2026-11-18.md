## Concrete editorial risks

- **POS separation:** `This bag is mine` and `That house is big` correctly illustrate *determiners*, but `This is mine` / `That's big` are *pronouns*. Do not use pronoun evidence to sign off a determiner sense.
- **Over-narrow distance:** Near/far is a useful beginner simplification, but `that` can refer to something near the listener or already mentioned. The current `that` definition is incomplete for broader use; the final A1 entry should either deliberately constrain itself to physical pointing or be separately reviewed for other senses.
- **Countability:** `this water` and `that music` are grammatical despite uncountable nouns. Do not falsely define either as requiring only singular countable nouns. The plural demonstrative determiners are `these` and `those`, not plural uses of `this`/`that`.
- **Myanmar deixis and register:** `ဒီ` is conversational, `ဤ` formal/written; `ဟို` implies a more remote referent, while `အဲဒီ` often identifies something already salient or near the listener. None is a mechanical one-to-one translation for all contexts. Avoid teaching `that` as always `ဟို`.
- **Cross-entry consistency:** Keep the same singular determiner distinction in both entries; do not conflate `that` determiner with `that` conjunction or relative marker. A cross-level sense audit is required before release.
