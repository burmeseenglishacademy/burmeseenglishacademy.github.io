## C1 copyright — noun, legal right over original work

- Shortlist index **807**, source `draft/A2-C1-EDITORIAL-STARTER.json`, source index **112**, source blob `b04dd85db5cfae131c25aa4c73109778991b6fdc`.
- Cambridge English Dictionary https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/copyright and Cambridge Learner's Dictionary https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/copyright (observed 2026-11-15) substantiate the **legal-right noun sense**, but the consulted visible entries **do not explicitly mark it C1**. The label 'Intermediate English' names a dictionary section, not a CEFR level; do **not** infer C1 from it.
- Candidate learner English: **A legal right that lets the owner control certain uses of an original creative work.**
- Candidate Myanmar: **မူရင်းဖန်တီးမှုလက်ရာတစ်ခုကို မိတ္တူကူးခြင်းနှင့် အသုံးပြုခြင်းအချို့အား ထိန်းချုပ်ခွင့်ပေးသည့် ဥပဒေဆိုင်ရာ မူပိုင်ခွင့်။**
- Original example (new editorial candidate): **The artist retained the copyright to her illustrations.**
- Grammar check: noun, uncountable in this example; `retain the copyright to` is a natural construction. Avoid conflating copyright ownership with ownership of a physical copy, and avoid treating copyright as a universal prohibition on all uses.
- **CEFR BLOCKER:** Obtain an authoritative, explicitly **C1** label for this exact legal-right noun sense or have independent reviewers adjudicate a replacement/level reassignment while maintaining exactly 200 unique valid senses at each level. Dictionary availability and specialist subject matter alone are not CEFR proof.
