## Proposed editorial resolution (not yet accepted)
**Preferred exact sense for this A1 entry:** main lexical/linking verb **be**, connecting a subject with its identity, role or quality; do not silently teach progressive or passive auxiliaries as the same A1 sense.

**Proposed English definition:** “To link someone or something to a description, identity or role.”

**Proposed fluent Myanmar explanation:** “လူတစ်ဦး သို့မဟုတ် အရာတစ်ခု၏ အမည်၊ အခြေအနေ၊ အရည်အသွေး သို့မဟုတ် အခန်းကဏ္ဍကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် သုံးသော ကြိယာ။” This is an explanatory gloss rather than a one-to-one Myanmar verb: English linking `be` often has no separately spoken Myanmar equivalent.

**Original teaching example:** “My sister wants to be a nurse.” / “ကျွန်တော့်အစ်မက သူနာပြု ဖြစ်ချင်တယ်။” The example contains the infinitive `to be` in a role/identity sense and does not imply an A2 progressive/passive auxiliary.

**Counterexamples for reviewer:** “She is reading.” (progressive auxiliary, Cambridge A2); “The window was broken.” (potential passive, Cambridge A2, but ambiguous with a result-state reading); “There is a shop nearby.” (existential, Cambridge A1, separate teaching use). The current broad “to exist” component should either be narrowed or explicitly reviewed as a distinct A1 use.
