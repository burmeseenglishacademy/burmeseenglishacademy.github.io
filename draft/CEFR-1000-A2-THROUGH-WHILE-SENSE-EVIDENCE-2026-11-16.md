## A2 `through` — preposition, source_index 80

Current provisional definition: “From one side or end of a place to the other.” Current Myanmar: “တစ်ဖက်မှ တစ်ဖက်သို့ ဖြတ်သန်း၍”. Existing example: “We walked through the park to reach the station.”

Evidence actually consulted: Cambridge Grammar Today, *Across, over or through?*, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/across-over-or-through (observed 2026-11-16). The source distinguishes movement **within/surrounded by** an area from crossing a surface, and identifies a British/American difference in the temporal use of `through`. This supports the spatial interpretation of the existing example but **does not substantiate CEFR A2**.

Proposed sense-specific English wording (unreviewed): “From one side of an area or enclosed space to the other, moving within it.” Proposed fluent Myanmar meaning (unreviewed): “နေရာတစ်ခု၏ အတွင်းဘက်ကို ဖြတ်၍ တစ်ဖက်မှ တစ်ဖက်သို့”. Original candidate example (unreviewed): “The children walked through the forest to reach the lake.” Keep the example spatial; do not conflate with American temporal `Monday through Friday`.

## A2 `while` — conjunction, source_index 81

Current provisional definition: “During the time that something else is happening.” Current Myanmar: “နေစဉ်၊ အချိန်အတွင်း”. Existing example: “I listened to music while I cooked dinner.”

Evidence actually consulted: Cambridge Grammar Today, *While and whilst*, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/while-and-whilst and *As, when or while?*, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/as-when-or-while (observed 2026-11-16). The sources explicitly distinguish simultaneous-time conjunction `while` from contrastive `while`, and distinguish `while` from point-in-time `when`. The Cambridge dictionary page https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/while was also observed on 2026-11-16 and includes a distinct noun sense; **the visible B1 tag for noun `a while` must not be transferred to the conjunction**. None of the observed grammar material establishes an exact-sense A2 rating.

Proposed sense-specific English wording (unreviewed): “During the time that another action or event is happening.” Proposed fluent Myanmar meaning (unreviewed): “တစ်ခုခု ဖြစ်ပျက်နေသည့် အချိန်အတွင်း၊ … နေစဉ်”. Original candidate example (unreviewed): “My sister prepared dinner while I washed the vegetables.” This tests simultaneous actions, not contrastive `while` or noun `a while`.
