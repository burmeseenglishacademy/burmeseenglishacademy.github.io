## resistance — B2 noun, opposition to change
Current English: “Refusal to accept or support a change or action.” Current Myanmar: “ခုခံဆန့်ကျင်မှု”. Current example: “The proposal met resistance from local residents.”

Cambridge main learner entry displays **C2** for an uncountable noun combining fighting against something and refusing to accept it: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/resistance (observed 2026-11-18). Cambridge Learner's Dictionary separately identifies disagreement with change, physical fighting, and resistance to illness, **without assigning a displayed CEFR level**: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/resistance (observed 2026-11-18). The C2 label applies to the displayed combined dictionary entry; it is a substantial warning, not conclusive proof that every separately distinguished use is C2.

**Candidate precision, NOT approved:** English: “Opposition to a proposed change or idea, especially when people do not want to accept it.” Myanmar: “အပြောင်းအလဲ သို့မဟုတ် အကြံပြုချက်တစ်ခုကို လက်မခံလို၍ ဆန့်ကျင်ခြင်း။” Original example: “The new parking rules faced resistance from residents.” The existing example is grammatical and demonstrates opposition to a proposal. Avoid importing medical immunity, electrical resistance, or armed resistance into this entry without separate sense review. **B2 versus C2 unresolved; retain hold.**
