## Language and example risk review

- **Affable:** distinguish approachable friendliness from politeness alone; original and alternative example grammar appear plausible, awaiting human signoff.
- **Adulation:** uncountable praise, often excessive; Myanmar should preserve the over-praise nuance.
- **Acrimonious:** bitter hostility in disagreement, not merely a loud or intense debate.
- **Culpability:** uncountable blameworthiness, not generic job responsibility.
- **Conjecture:** noun can be countable or uncountable; distinguish speculative explanation from a formal mathematical conjecture.
- **Disparage:** requires an object; disrespectful criticism, not any constructive criticism.
- **Misconstrue:** transitive; `misconstrue X as Y` is an important construction, with no necessary deliberate deceit.
- **Irrevocable:** especially decisions and commitments; avoid suggesting that all physical changes are legally irrevocable.
