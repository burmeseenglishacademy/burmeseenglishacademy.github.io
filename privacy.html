Privacy & Your Data

Learn with confidence. No login, no saved learner progress. Hosting privacy limits are explained clearly.

Reviewed draft: 22 October 2026 · Contact: learnersbea@gmail.com

Our plain-language privacy promise

Learn English without creating an account. The Academy does not ask for your name, email address or password, and does not deliberately collect or keep completed-lesson records, quiz history, writing drafts or voice recordings on its servers. Practice answers exist temporarily in the current page only and disappear when you refresh or leave. We do not install analytics or advertising trackers.

အကောင့်ဖွင့်ခြင်း၊ Password ထည့်ခြင်း မလိုအပ်ပါ။ Academy က သင်ခန်းစာပြီးဆုံးမှု၊ Quiz ရလဒ်မှတ်တမ်း၊ Writing Draft နဲ့ အသံဖိုင်တွေကို Server ပေါ် မသိမ်းပါ။ လက်ရှိ Page မှာ လေ့ကျင့်နေစဉ် ယာယီပေါ်နေတဲ့ အချက်အလက်တွေက Refresh လုပ်ခြင်း သို့မဟုတ် Page ထွက်ခြင်းနဲ့ ပျောက်သွားနိုင်ပါတယ်။ Academy က ကြော်ငြာနဲ့ Analytics Tracking မထည့်ထားပါ။

What information is processed?

InformationHow it is handled
Accounts and credentialsNone. No sign-up or login.
Lesson completion and quiz historyNot saved by the Academy. Quiz feedback appears only in the active page.
Writing entriesProcessed in the browser for word count and basic local feedback. Not uploaded or automatically saved. Copy work before leaving.
Microphone recordingsOptional. Created temporarily within the active Speaking page using browser recording APIs. Not uploaded by the Academy. Click Delete recording or leave the page to clear app-held data.
English voicesProvided by your device/browser. Availability and possible third-party network processing depend on your browser/OS vendor. The Academy does not operate a paid voice API.
Speech-to-textDisabled because browser services can send microphone audio for remote processing.
Web traffic / IP addressGitHub Pages, our hosting provider, logs visitor IP addresses for security and may process technical request information under its own policies. This is outside the Academy website's direct control.
Cookies and local storageThis edition does not write Academy learning data or preferences to cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. Older Academy versions may have left localStorage entries; see below.

အရေးကြီးချက် — GitHub Pages က Website လည်ပတ်မှုနဲ့ လုံခြုံရေးအတွက် လာရောက်ကြည့်ရှုသူ၏ IP Address ကို Log သိမ်းနိုင်ပါတယ်။ Academy ကိုယ်တိုင် Visitor Tracking Tool မထည့်ထားပါ။ အချို့ Browser အသံစနစ်တွေက Online Service ကို အသုံးပြုနိုင်ပါတယ်။

Clear your current session and old saved progress

Refresh or close the learning page to clear current writing, quiz answers and temporary practice data. For recordings, use Delete recording while in Speaking, or leave that page. This website cannot delete voice files that you have saved elsewhere or technical logs controlled by GitHub.

Earlier Academy versions saved learning progress and voice settings in localStorage. This version neither reads nor updates those records, but they can remain on your device until you remove them. Use the button below to remove known Academy storage keys in this browser only. It does not clear other applications' data.

ယခင် Version က Browser ထဲသိမ်းထားတဲ့ Progress တွေရှိနိုင်ပါတယ်။ အပေါ်က Button ကို နှိပ်ပြီး ဖျက်နိုင်ပါတယ်။ ယခုလေ့လာနေတဲ့ Page ကို Refresh လုပ်ရင် လက်ရှိ လေ့ကျင့်မှုအချက်အလက်တွေ ပျောက်သွားမှာပါ။

Hosting and third parties

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for hosting logs and privacy rights. Links to third-party English or IELTS resources open only if you choose to visit them; their own policies then apply.

No Academy-owned cloud learner database, accounts, advertising analytics, social login, or voice-upload endpoint operates on this release. We do not sell learning data or use your voice recordings for AI training.

Questions and requests

Contact learnersbea@gmail.com for privacy or site questions. Because the Academy does not operate learner accounts or a learning-history database, we cannot retrieve an individual learner's progress or remotely erase provider logs. Do not send sensitive personal information or voice recordings unless specifically necessary for your request.
