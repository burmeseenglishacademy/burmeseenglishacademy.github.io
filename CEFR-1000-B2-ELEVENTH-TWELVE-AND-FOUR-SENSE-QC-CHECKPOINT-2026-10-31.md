## Four directly inspected Oxford dictionary sense leads

- [Graduate (verb)](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/graduate_2), lines 110–125: university-degree completion with `graduate from` in British English. Distinguish the North American direct-object construction and the noun `graduate`.
- [Appointment (noun)](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/appointment), lines 105–115 and 148–162: countable arranged medical visit versus appointment to an office or job. The dental booking in the frozen example is the first sense.
- [Contemporary (adjective)](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/contemporary_1), lines 105–143: present-day art is sense 2, different from belonging to the same historical period.
- [Raw (adjective)](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/raw_1), lines 107–139: uncooked chicken is food sense 1; do not mix with unprocessed materials/data, raw emotion or painful skin.

**Unresolved Oxford publisher-surface inconsistency:** the four opened dictionary pages each displayed an **Oxford 5000 C1** footer (graduate lines 241–245, appointment 250–254, contemporary 181–185, raw 236–240), while the official Oxford 3000 PDF explicitly places the same word/POS at B1 or B2. These footer badges must not be silently treated as verified exact-sense C1 labels. They may reflect a page-template or source-scope difference; independent publisher clarification or reviewer adjudication is required.
